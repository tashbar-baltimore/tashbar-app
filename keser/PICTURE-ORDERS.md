# Keser Girls 3.0 · picture orders

How this works (`CLAUDE.md`, "Shared art library"; `art/README.md`): the Keser builder writes the orders, the HoS's image account makes them (orders go by email; **the address is never written in the repository**), the builder checks every picture against its order and the rules, converts it to WebP (at most 1,100 px wide, at most 200 KB), keeps the original in `art/originals/`, and adds it by pull request with a row in `art/CATALOG.md` that says **"Keser Girls only"** in "what it shows". Licence: "made for Tashbar Baltimore with an AI image tool, owned by the school". Until a picture arrives, the screen uses coded 3D or drawn shapes, never an empty box.

## The Keser style (every picture)
- **Look:** a premium 3D animated-film look, softly lit, glossy and elegant: classy, not cartoonish. Jewel tones: rose, lilac, champagne gold, mint, with deep plum shadows. Soft glow, a little glitter bokeh, gold-foil accents. Clean, airy, one clear subject.
- **No words, letters or numbers anywhere in the picture.** No brand names or logos.
- **People:** girls and women only (Keser Girls only), modest and frum in every detail. Girls: dresses or skirts well below the knee, sleeves past the elbow, high necklines, neat hair (ponytail, braids, headband, bow). Married women: hair fully covered (sheitel, snood or tichel), modest dresses below the knee, sleeves past the elbow. No boys or men.
- Warm frum family life; kosher food only. No church, cross or other religions' symbols. No Divine Name.
