## shapes-c: Flat or solid?, Build with shapes, Where is it?, Fix the pattern, Pattern train, Sort by color

| Game | What was wrong (rule) | What changed | Pictures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flat or solid? | The "flat" shapes were thick riveted space panels and the solids were a striped planet, a swirly ball, a traffic cone and a striped can: a child could not see flat vs solid (1, 6, 9). Box labels were a tiny white square and a small yellow cube. | Plain one-colour pieces (`plain: true`; kit now also keeps solids plain): flat ones are thin like card, solids are smooth shaded solids. | Build with shapes | Lesson triangles were striped beach-towel cloth with white edges; pieces were sand-textured, sand on sand (1, 9). The holes were faint dashed lines on a pale board (9). Each hole is a deeper colour with a dark edge (the hint flashes the edge gold). | Where is it? | Moving light lines (caustics) danced over the whole sea floor (10). The bridge legs were studded with little pearls that looked like more balls (8, 11). The plain block bridge (red legs, blue top). The round thing is always "the ball". The ball starts in from the corner and is a clear lemon yellow (it looked mustard).
