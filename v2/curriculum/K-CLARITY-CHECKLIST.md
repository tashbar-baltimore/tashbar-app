| Flat or solid? | The "flat" shapes were thick riveted space panels and the solids were a striped planet, a swirly ball, a traffic cone and a striped can: a child could not see flat vs solid (1, 6, 9). Box labels were a tiny white square and a small yellow cube. | Plain one-colour pieces (`plain: true`; kit now also keeps solids plain): flat ones are thin like card, solids are smooth shaded solids. Box labels are big: a flat blue square, and a solid blue cube turned so three faces show (from the front a cube looked like a square). | `flat-or-solid/` |
| Build with shapes | Lesson triangles were striped beach-towel cloth with white edges; pieces were sand-textured, sand on sand (1, 9). | Plain bright pieces in different colours on the plain board, so the outline of each hole and piece is what he sees. | `build-shapes/` |
| Where is it? | Moving light lines (caustics) danced over the whole sea floor (10). The bridge legs were studded with little pearls that looked like more balls (8, 11). The voice said "pearl" in the lesson and "sphere" in the game for the same ball (11). The ball to drag sat in the screen corner, stretched (9). | No moving light lines (calm). The plain block bridge (red legs, blue top). The round thing is always "the ball". The ball starts in from the corner. **(decided: "ball", not "sphere" or "pearl": the game teaches position words, so every other word is the most familiar one.)** | `where-is-it/` |
| Sort by color | Every piece had carved-leaf texture; the yellow box looked orange; the busy leafy mat under the shapes (9, 10, 11). Bonus round had a teal piece with no box: wrong drop said "It goes in the a new color box." | Plain one-colour pieces; a lemon yellow; the painted stage mat (all worlds) is paler and less colourful, like the painting behind. Every bonus piece now has its box (new shapes, same three colours: "New shapes! Look only at the color."). | `sort-colors/` |
