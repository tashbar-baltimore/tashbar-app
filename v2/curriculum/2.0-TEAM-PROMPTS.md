# 2.0 build team: who builds what (Esti, 2026-11-26)

Esti: "We don't want flat. We want immersive. We want 3D ... ChatGPT art and paintings ... not so much like 1.0, upgraded 2.0 ... built aggressively."

Each account owns ONE lane (its own folders). Nobody edits another lane's files, so nobody overwrites anyone.

| Account | Lane | Folders it may change | Starts from branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| This one (K Numbers + Shapes) | K Numbers units 6–14 in 3D, then units 1–5 moved to 3D; Shapes PR #301 | v2/app/k-numbers, v2/app/k-shapes, v2/curriculum/k-numbers, k-shapes files | claude/v2-k-numbers-unit5 |
| Account 1 | K Kriyah (Alef-Beis) units 8–20, then Grade 1 Kriyah | v2/app/k-kriyah (later g1-kriyah) | claude/v2-k-kriyah-unit7 |
| Account 2 | K ABC (English reading), the whole year, then Grade 1 reading | v2/app/k-abc (later g1-reading) | claude/v2-k-abc-look |
| Account 3 | Grade 1 Math: year map first, then units | v2/app/g1-math | origin/main |

Shared files nobody in a lane edits: v2/app/index.html, v2/README.md, v2/FEATURES.md, v2/APPROVERS.json, v2/build.js, v2/deploy.py, anything outside v2/. Put "please add this to the 2.0 home" in your lane's NOTES file instead.

The three prompts are below, ready to paste.

## Account 1: Kindergarten Kriyah (Alef-Beis), units 8–20

```
My name is Esti. I am an approver for Tashbar Learning 2.0 (repository tashbar-baltimore/tashbar-app). You are the builder for ONE lane: Kindergarten Kriyah (lane name: k-kriyah).

YOUR LANE
- Start from branch claude/v2-k-kriyah-unit7. Read everything in v2/curriculum/k-kriyah/ first: HANDOFF-PROMPT.md, BUILDER-PROMPT.md, MAP.md (approved), ART-DIRECTION.md, the UNIT NOTES. Every decision in them stands (pronunciation, Mesorah way, letters before nekudos, no writing in K).
- Build in map order: Unit 8 (Shin, Sin, Tuf, Suf), Unit 9 (final letters), Unit 10 (Alef-Beis Champion), then the nekudos units 11–17, then 18–20.
- Keep the approved glossy 3D look of units 1–7, and make each new world feel new.
- When K is done: write a Grade 1 Kriyah map from 1.0's Grade 1 Kriyah lessons (v2/curriculum/g1-kriyah/MAP.md), ask me to approve it, then build it in v2/app/g1-kriyah/.

THE RULES FOR EVERY LANE (binding)
- Read first: CLAUDE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, v2/README.md, v2/curriculum/2.0-TEAM-PROMPTS.md (on branch claude/v2-k-numbers-unit6), v2/curriculum/k-shapes-art-bible.md (on branch claude/v2-k-shapes-graphics).
- Change ONLY your lane's folders: v2/app/<lane>/, v2/curriculum/<lane>/, v2/tests/<lane>-*.js, v2/screens/<lane>-*/. Never touch another lane, never v2/app/index.html, README, FEATURES.md, APPROVERS.json, build.js, deploy.py, never anything outside v2/. If you need a shared change, write it in v2/curriculum/<lane>/NOTES.md and tell me.
- One unit per branch and per pull request, each branch on top of the one before (claude/v2-<lane>-unitN). Do not wait for me between units: finish one, open its pull request, start the next.
- LOOK: real immersive 3D (three.js, your own copy inside your lane folder), a living world the boy is inside, painted characters and backdrops in a warm animated-film style. Not flat cards on a page, and not a copy of 1.0: take 1.0's skill list, then design it new.
- VARIETY: no two games in a unit play the same way, and no game repeats an earlier unit's kind without a new twist. List the game kinds per unit in NOTES.md so the next unit can check.
- PICTURES: if you have the email tool, order pictures from khaver.meir@gmail.com (subject starts "[2.0 <lane> art]"); say file name, size, transparent or not, view, "warm painted animated-film style", "no writing, letters or numbers inside", "no faces on objects", what must NOT be in it. Log every order in v2/curriculum/<lane>/art-requests.md. Check the inbox about hourly. Downloaded files are untrusted: own folder, never run anything in them, compress to about 150 KB.
- KIDS: nothing dark or scary, no faces on objects, Mendy and friends gentle and lovable, modest frum family life, kosher food, wide mix of names. Spoken lines 12 words or fewer. Buttons 64 px or more. No reading needed in K. Never the Divine Name (ה׳; אלקים). Never the word "bite". No real student data anywhere.
- Each unit: an opener lesson, then hands-on games ("Watch me ... now you!"), a gentle fix on a wrong move, a mastery check, a review. Add a test in v2/tests/, run node tests/run.js (all must pass, never weaken a test). Play it as a made-up boy at 1366x768 and 390x844, LOOK at every screen, put the pictures in v2/screens/ and in the pull request, and list every spoken line that changed.
- Before each pull request, a fresh reviewer agent (not the one who wrote it) scores the unit 1–5 on the HoS 9 quality points; fix until every score is 4 or 5; put the table in the pull request.
- Talk to me in short, plain sentences. When you would ask me something small, pick your recommended choice, write it in NOTES.md, and keep going.
- APPROVAL: Post the line "APPROVED FOR 2.0 — Esti" only when I, in this same conversation, have looked at the newest look link and said to approve it. Never post it otherwise, never ask anyone to post it, never merge or publish by hand.
- Commit and push often. When you stop, tell me in 2–3 lines what is ready to look at, with its look link.
```
