## What changed (short)

**Shapes**
- Every first meeting with a shape is a plain, flat, one-colour shape: no drums, Lego bricks, striped sails, cloud paint or woven baskets. Real things come later.
- Every corner gets its own marker, and every side lights one by one when the voice talks about sides.
- Calm worlds: no butterflies, sparkles or scattered props; the painting behind is paler. The game's name is always on screen.
- Comparisons can't be argued: big and small are the same thing in two sizes; Heavy and light is a real balloon against a rock; in Fix the shape the right stick now LOOKS exactly as long as the gap (before, it looked 30% longer).
- Solids are plain solids, standing up; an upside-down cone shows its round base, so it never looks like a flat triangle.

**Numbers**
- No apples hang on the orchard trees (only the apples to count), the camera comes in close, no apple hides behind another, the see-saw apples sit in rows of five.
- Unit 2 stones show their own amount (rows of dots), not five identical stars. No eyes on cloths and towels. Bigger numbers and cards to tap. The game's name is always on screen.

**ABC and Kriyah**
- Calm worlds (no grass blades, butterflies or floating sparkles), small rewards instead of confetti storms, the game's name on screen.
- ABC Unit 3: the n hunt no longer has butterflies carrying pictures (they sit still; a basket catches them); the tracing path is a white road with dark dots you can actually see; each "Meet" game shows the plain book letter beside the picture-letter; unclear pictures fixed or left out (the dish looked like a plate, the mop like a cupcake liner).

## Choices made for you (a kindergarten teacher's choices; tell us if you want different)

- Shapes: the first games for each shape use plain flat shapes for the whole game, not only the lesson. Real things ("a tire is a circle") come only in the later games.
- Shapes: cupcakes stay in the pattern games (the voice says cupcakes and every child knows them). "Like a can" and "like a block" stay in the solid-shape lessons.
- Shapes: the drum and bell stay in the sound pattern (they make the sounds; they are not shapes).
- Numbers: Unit 2 stones show dots in rows of five, not written numbers (numbers are met in Unit 4).
- Numbers: the quick "flash" stays in the Unit 1 check and the Peek and Finger flash games: seeing a small amount at a glance is the skill.
- Numbers: the Unit 6 path is one straight line 0 to 10 across the screen. Apple trees have leaves but no apples. On a phone the see-saw holds rows of three apples (rows of five would make them tiny).
- Wrong-move help goes from least to most: the first miss shows how, the second says the exact fix.
