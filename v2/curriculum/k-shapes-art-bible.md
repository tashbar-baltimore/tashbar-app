# Kindergarten Shapes: the art bible

**What Esti asked for (2026-11-26), in one line:** every world's objects become real, lovable things from that world (cupcakes in the bakery, a tractor tire on the farm), each one clearly the shape being taught; the characters are redrawn to be gentle and lovable (or left out); nothing is scary; the whole thing looks like a top children's animated film.

Every picture we order and every object we build follows this page. If something is not covered here, choose the softer, calmer, warmer option.

## 1. The rules that come before beauty (teaching first)
1. **The shape first, the object second.** A piece is seen face-on. Its outline is the exact shape the game always used (`KINDS` in `kit.js`): straight sides really straight, corners really corners, curves really round. The world only changes the *paint* (icing, wood, tread, cloth), never the outline. A boy counting corners counts the true number.
2. **Nothing sticks out of the outline.** No stems, handles or toppers that change a flat shape's outline. (Solids may have a tiny stem or lid only if the solid still reads as the solid.)
3. **The shape is named, the shape glows.** When the voice names a shape in a lesson, a soft glowing outline of the pure shape shows on the object.
4. **Tricky look-alikes stay exactly as tricky** (oval vs circle, leaning shape vs rectangle, curvy vs triangle, kite vs square). Same outline, same answer.
5. **Colour sorting:** the object's main colour is clearly its colour word (red icing, blue sail). Details are white, cream or gold so they never compete.
6. **Patterns change ONE feature only.** Frosting colour, or the cookie's shape, never both. Everything else (case, cake, size) is identical.
7. **Sizes on screen:** a piece is at least as big as before (about 90 px across on a Chromebook, 60 px on a phone). Buttons stay 64 px or more. No reading needed.

## 2. Kid-friendly means
- Nothing scary: no dark scenes, sharp teeth, angry or staring faces, glowing eyes, shadows that look like figures, sudden loud flashes, shaking screen, blades, fire (a warm oven glow is fine), deep water or falling.
- **No faces on objects.** Only Mendy and his friends have faces.
- Soft, rounded, warm, bright, happy. Calm motion. If a 5-year-old might be startled, change it.
- Modest, warm, frum family life. Kosher-looking food only (no meat with dairy). No other religions' symbols (no Christmas trees, ornaments, crosses, Easter eggs).
- Never write the Divine Name. Never the word "bite". No writing, letters or numbers inside any picture.

## 3. The look
| | |
|---|---|
| **Style** | A warm painted children's animated film: rounded, soft, glossy where it is candy or toy, matte where it is cloth, wood or dough. |
| **Light** | One warm key light from the upper left (sun or window), a soft sky fill, a gentle warm rim from behind. No hard black shadows. |
| **Shadows** | Soft, coloured (a piece's shadow carries a little of its colour), never pure black. |
| **Outline** | Painted pictures: a thin, soft, darker version of the object's own colour (never black). 3D objects: no outline; a white piped edge or bevel catches the light instead. |
| **Corners** | Every corner is very slightly softened by a bevel (it still counts as a corner and looks like one). |
| **Motion** | Slow and gentle: bobbing, breathing, a small turn. Celebrations float and settle; nothing flashes faster than twice a second. |
| **Depth** | The far world is softly blurred, like a camera focused on the toys. |

## 5. Characters
- **Mendy:** a gentle, lovable 5-year-old. Natural child proportions (head a little large, never a doll). Warm brown eyes of normal size with a small highlight (no big glassy whites, never staring), soft raised brows, rosy cheeks, a small kind closed smile. Light-brown hair with a soft fringe, a navy knitted kippah, tzitzis, a light-blue long-sleeved shirt, navy trousers. Calm idle (breathing, a blink, a small head tilt), a happy hop, a clap, a gentle point.
- **Friends** (Shmuel, Eli, Mordechai ...): the same style, each with his own hair and shirt colour; a wide mix of looks.
- **Mommy and Tatty:** not on the play screens (Esti, 2026-11-26). They may come back only at the very end, small, lovingly drawn (Mommy: tichel, long sleeves, long skirt; Tatty: hat, beard, jacket), and only after Esti approves how they look (`?parents=1` shows them for her to judge).
- Two ways are built for Esti to choose: **3D** (made in code, gentle redesign) and **painted** (illustrated pictures shown as flat cards in the 3D world).

## 6. Picture orders (email account)
Every order states: the file name; the size; transparent PNG or not; the view (face-on, top-down, side); "the same warm painted animated-film style as the backdrops"; "no writing, letters or numbers"; "no faces on objects"; and what must NOT be in it. Characters: 1024 px tall, transparent. Objects: 512 x 512, transparent, the true shape face-on. Mats: 1024 x 1024 top-down. Saved compressed (about 150 KB or less each) in `v2/app/k-shapes/art/`. Every order and answer is logged in `k-shapes-art-requests.md`.

## 7. Speed
A whole game loads in a few seconds on a school Chromebook. 3D treasures are made in code (no download). Painted pictures are compressed and loaded only by the games that use them. The automatic test (`?fast=1`) keeps the plain pieces so it stays quick.
