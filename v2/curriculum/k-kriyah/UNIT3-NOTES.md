## Decisions I made on my own (each one line, so you can say yes or change it)
1. **Vov's picture is a hook.** "Vov" means a hook, and Mommy hangs her apron on one. The boy hears "Vov says v, just like Veis!"
2. **Zayin's picture is zemiros, shown as music notes,** on Friday night with Shabbos candles lit. Ches's picture is the challah.
3. **Mommy** wears a long dress with long sleeves and a neat sheitel. Ari is the Unit 3 boy.
4. **What to look for:**
   - Zayin has a crown on top; Vov has no crown.
   - Hei's little leg doesn't touch the roof; Ches's leg touches the roof.

   These are the same words in every game. An orange ring circles the place.
5. **The mastery check never asks "Which letter says v?" with both Vov and Veis on screen,** since both say v.
6. **The review round is Sound Match for Unit 2's letters,** and Feel the Shape ends with Hei, Ches's look-alike.
7. **The computer voice says "khh" for Ches.** A computer can't really say ח, so this one needs the rebbe's recording most of all.
8. **The Look-Alike Detective for these pairs is a new game.** It has two labelled crates instead of Unit 1's houses, because "someone home" belongs to Beis and Veis only.

## For the rebbe (every sound and rule used in Unit 3)
- **Letters:** Vov (ו) says "v", Zayin (ז) says "z", Ches (ח) says "ch" (as in challah). These are their own lone sounds, never with a vowel.
- **Look-alikes:** Zayin has a crown and Vov does not. Ches's left leg touches the roof; Hei's does not.
- **Words:** vov (a hook), challah, zemiros, Shabbos, Friday night.
- **Shown:** the zemiros scene shows lit Shabbos candles and music notes only. No bracha, no pasuk and no Divine Name.
- **Chant order:** Alef, Beis, Veis, Gimmel, Daled, Hei, Vov, Zayin, Ches.
- **Temporary:** the letter sounds are the computer voice until the rebbe's recording is in. Ches especially.
