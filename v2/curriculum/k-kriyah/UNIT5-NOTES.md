## Decisions I made on my own (each one line, so you can say yes or change it)
1. **The story is a picnic with Bubby,** not Chanukah: limon (lemon), mayim (water), nemalah (ant).
2. **Eli** is the Unit 5 boy. **Bubby** wears a light sheitel and a long dress.
3. **What to look for, in the same words in every game:**
   - Gimmel has a foot; Nun is flat at the bottom.
   - Tes is closed at the bottom; Mem has an opening at the bottom.
   - Lamed is the tall one.
4. **The Train carries the newest six letters (Yud to Nun),** not all 16. A 16-car train is too long for a phone and for a 5-year-old.
5. **The review round is Sound Match for Unit 4's letters.**

## For the rebbe (every sound and rule used in Unit 5)
- **Letters:** Lamed (ל) says "l", Mem (מ) says "m", Nun (נ) says "n". These are lone sounds, never with a vowel.
- **Look-alikes:** Gimmel has a foot at the bottom; Nun is flat at the bottom. Tes is closed at the bottom; Mem has an opening at the bottom-left.
- **Words:** limon (lemon), mayim (water), nemalah (ant), picnic.
- **Shown:** a picnic blanket and a basket; nothing from Shabbos or Yom Tov.
- **Chant order:** Alef, Beis, Veis, Gimmel, Daled, Hei, Vov, Zayin, Ches, Tes, Yud, Kaf, Chaf, Lamed, Mem, Nun.
- **Temporary:** the letter sounds are the computer voice until the rebbe's recording is in.
