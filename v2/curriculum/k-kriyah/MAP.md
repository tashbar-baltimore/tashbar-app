# Kindergarten Kriyah — the full-year map (DRAFT, waiting for approval)

**The goal:** "Fun, easy, skill-building games that teach every kind of boy real skills, one step on top of the next, all year, until he graduates."

## Decisions so far (from the approver)
- **Letters first, nekudos later.** A boy learns every letter's **name** and its **own sound** (Beis says "b", Shin says "sh"). Nekudos start only when he knows the whole Alef-Beis very, very well.
- **A letter never says "Sha" or "Shaw" until nekudos come.** Before that, Shin only says "sh".
- **Alef and Ayin are quiet.** They wait for their nekudah. He still learns that Abba starts with an Alef.
- **Pronunciation:** Kometz says "aw", Pasach "ah", Segol "eh", Tzeirei "A" (as in "day"), Chirik "ee", Cholam "O", Kubutz and Shuruk "oo". ת without a dagesh is **Suf**.
- **Nekudos are taught the Mesorah way:** nekudah, letter, sound ("Kometz, Beis, baw").
- **Look-alike letters** are taught apart first, then compared side by side with the difference colored.
- **Final letters** are matched to their regular partners.
- **No writing in Kindergarten.** Ksav yad comes in a later year. The letter games are touch, drag and match only.
- **Listening for the first sound of a word** ("Shabbos" starts with "Sha") comes with the nekudos, not before.
- **The voice is the rebbe's own recording** for every letter, nekudah and sound.

## How the year fits together
| Part | Units | About how long |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The Alef-Beis: names and sounds** | 1–10 | Sept – Jan (about 18 weeks) |
| **B. The nekudos, one at a time** | 11–17 | Feb – Apr (about 12 weeks) |
| **C. Reading chunks and first words** | 18–19 | May – June (about 5 weeks) |
| **D. Graduation** | 20 | Last week |

**The letter order** is Alef-Beis order, the way the boys sing it and hear it in cheder. Most look-alike pairs already come far apart in that order: ב–כ, ד–ר, ג–נ, ט–מ, ס–ם. Only ו–ז and ה–ח come close together, and those units get extra comparison games.

**Every day, about 10–15 minutes:** one new game of 2–4 minutes, then a short mixed review of letters from earlier units.

**Review comes back all year.**
- Every game ends with 2–3 items from earlier units.
- Every unit ends with a review round of everything learned so far.
- Units 10 and 17 are big whole-part reviews.
- Something he got wrong comes back sooner. Something he knows well comes back later.

**Mastery:** the next game opens at 2 of 3 stars. If a boy struggles, the game quietly gets easier: fewer letters, a hint glow, and the sound played again. A quick boy gets a "bonus round" with more letters, mixed in faster.

## The game kinds (the same few all year, so boys learn how to play them once)
Each game starts by showing how to play: the voice says "Watch me… now you!" while a hand does one example.

| Game | What the boy does | Teaching method | Helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Picture Story** (each unit opener) | Hears a short story. The picture turns into the letter (the shofar curls into a ש), then the plain letter stays. He taps it to hear its name and sound. | Picture inside the letter (Shmidman & Ehri 2010, tested on Hebrew letters) | Seeing, story, slow |
| **Feel the Shape** | Runs his finger along the letter's shape on screen while its name and sound play. This is for feeling the shape, not for writing. | Feeling letters while learning them (Bara & Gentaz 2004, 2007) | Touching, moving |
| **Letter Hunt** | Finds every Beis hidden in a picture: a sukkah, a shul, the Shabbos table. | Picking it out from other letters | Seeing, quick learners |
| **Sound Match** | Drags pictures to the letter whose sound they start with: the shofar goes to ש. | Linking the letter and its sound | Hearing, seeing |
| **Look-Alike Detective** | Two letters side by side, with the difference glowing in color (Beis's little tail). He sorts cards into the right box. | Look-alikes taught apart, then compared (Carnine 1976, 1981) | Seeing, slow |
| **Find the Mistake** | "Yaakov says this is a Daled. Is he right?" He taps yes or no, then fixes it by dragging the right letter in. | Mistakes that teach | Everyone |
| **Alef-Beis Train** | Puts letter-cars on the train in Alef-Beis order while the Alef-Beis chant plays. | Order, rhythm, song | Music, moving |
| **Partner Match** (finals) | Joins each final letter to its regular partner: ם to מ. | Matching pairs | Seeing |
| **Nekudah Builder** (Part B) | Drags a nekudah under a letter and hears "Kometz, Beis, baw". | Letter + nekudah read as one piece (Share & Bar-On 2018) | Hearing, moving |
| **First Sound** (Part B) | Hears "Shabbos" and drags it to שַׁ. | Hebrew-speaking children hear the first beat most easily (Frontiers 2019) | Hearing |
| **Reading Path** (Part C) | Walks a path tapping each chunk as he reads it; it lights up, then he hears the whole word. | Reading smoothly, right to left | Moving, slow |
| **Word Builder** (Part C) | Drags chunks in order to build שַׁבָּת, then hears it. | Building words | Moving, quick learners |

**Mastery check** at the end of each unit: short "hear it, tap it" questions, mixed with earlier letters. Tap-to-check is allowed for checks.

## Part A — The Alef-Beis: names and sounds

### Unit 1. Alef, Beis, Veis (א בּ ב) — 3 weeks, a slow start
- **Skill:** knows Alef (quiet), Beis ("b") and Veis ("v"), and learns how the games work.
- **Story:** Mendy's first day in cheder. Tatty walks him there and the rebbe shows him the first letter.
- **Games:** Picture Story (Abba → א; bayis → בּ), Feel the Shape, Letter Hunt, Look-Alike Detective (Beis and Veis: the dot glows), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** names and sounds all three, and tells Beis from Veis 2 times out of 3.

### Unit 2. Gimmel, Daled, Hei (ג ד ה) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** names and sounds ג "g", ד "d", ה "h".
- **Story:** Shloimy and Zeidy pick grapes (gefen) and go fishing (dag).
- **Games:** Picture Story, Feel the Shape, Sound Match, Alef-Beis Train (א to ה), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** all 6 letters so far, mixed.

### Unit 3. Vov, Zayin, Ches (ו ז ח) — 2 weeks, look-alike unit
- **Skill:** names and sounds ו "v", ז "z", ח "ch". Tells ו from ז and ה from ח.
- **Story:** Ari helps Mommy bake challah for Shabbos. The family sings zemiros.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Look-Alike Detective (ו/ז: Zayin's crown glows; ה/ח: Hei's gap glows), Letter Hunt, Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** sorts ו/ז and ה/ח correctly, plus everything learned so far.

### Unit 4. Tes, Yud, Kaf, Chaf (ט י כּ כ) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** ט "t", י "y", כּ "k", כ "ch". Tells כ from ב: Beis has a tail at the bottom, Kaf is round.
- **Story:** Dovid sees Tatty's tallis in shul. He makes Kiddush with Tatty's kos.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Feel the Shape, Look-Alike Detective (ב/כ), Sound Match, Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** all letters so far, mixed.

### Unit 5. Lamed, Mem, Nun (ל מ נ) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** ל "l", מ "m", נ "n". Tells ג from נ and ט from מ.
- **Story:** Eli and Bubby light the Chanukah candles (ner) and eat latkes.
- **Games:** Picture Story (lulav, matzah, ner), Look-Alike Detective (ג/נ, ט/מ), Alef-Beis Train (א to נ), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** all letters so far, mixed.

### Unit 6. Samech, Ayin, Pei, Fei (ס ע פּ פ) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** ס "s", ע quiet, פּ "p", פ "f".
- **Story:** Avi builds the sukkah with Tatty. A tree (eitz) shades it.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Feel the Shape, Look-Alike Detective (Pei and Fei: the dot glows), Sound Match, Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** all letters so far, mixed.

### Unit 7. Tzadik, Kuf, Reish (צ ק ר) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** צ "tz", ק "k", ר "r". Tells ד from ר: Daled has a corner, Reish is round.
- **Story:** Tzvi checks his tzitzis. Zeidy makes Kiddush. A rimon on Rosh Hashanah.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Look-Alike Detective (ד/ר), Letter Hunt, Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** all letters so far, mixed.

### Unit 8. Shin, Sin, Tuf, Suf (שׁ שׂ תּ ת) — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** שׁ "sh", שׂ "s", תּ "t", ת "s". Tells ה, ח and ת apart.
- **Story:** Moshe hears the shofar in shul. Simchas Torah dancing with the Torah.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Look-Alike Detective (Shin and Sin: the dot glows; ה/ח/ת side by side), Alef-Beis Train (the whole Alef-Beis), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** the whole Alef-Beis, mixed.

### Unit 9. The final letters (ך ם ן ף ץ) — 1 week
- **Skill:** knows each final letter, its partner, and that it says the same sound. Tells ס from ם.
- **Story:** Chaim learns that some letters wear "Shabbos clothes" at the end of a word.
- **Games:** Picture Story, Partner Match, Look-Alike Detective (ס/ם, ו/ן), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** matches all 5 pairs and names each final letter.

### Unit 10. Alef-Beis Champion — 2 weeks, the big review and the gate to nekudos
- **Skill:** knows every letter's name and sound, quickly and surely, in any order.
- **Games:** mixed rounds of every game kind, look-alike rounds, and Alef-Beis Train against a gentle clock (bonus round only).
- **Mastery (the gate):** names and sounds every letter in a mixed check, nearly all right, on **two different days**. Nekudos open only after this. A boy who isn't there yet gets extra review rounds that focus on the letters he misses.

## Part B — The nekudos, one at a time
Every unit works the same way:
1. **Opener:** a nekudah story ("Kometz is a little table under the letter…").
2. **Nekudah Builder** with known letters, a few at a time, then all of them.
3. **First Sound** listening game.
4. **Look-Alike Detective** for nekudos that look or sound close.
5. **Find the Mistake.**
6. **Mastery check**, then a review of letters and earlier nekudos.

| Unit | Nekudah | Sound | Special focus | About |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Kometz ◌ָ | aw | the first one; the Mesorah way, "Kometz, Beis, baw" | 2 weeks |
| 12 | Pasach ◌ַ | ah | **Kometz and Pasach side by side**; they sound close, so extra time | 2 weeks |
| 13 | Segol ◌ֶ | eh | 3 dots in a triangle | 1.5 weeks |
| 14 | Tzeirei ◌ֵ | A | Segol and Tzeirei side by side (3 dots vs 2) | 1.5 weeks |
| 15 | Chirik ◌ִ | ee | one dot underneath | 1.5 weeks |
| 16 | Cholam ◌ֹ | O | the dot on top; don't mix it up with Shin's dot | 1.5 weeks |
| 17 | Kubutz ◌ֻ and Shuruk וּ | oo | two looks, one sound; then a **big review of all nekudos** | 2 weeks |

**Mastery for each nekudah:** reads it with every letter in a mixed check. Kometz/Pasach and Segol/Tzeirei are checked together.

## Part C — Reading chunks and first words

### Unit 18. Two chunks, smoothly — 2 weeks
- **Skill:** reads two letter-and-nekudah chunks in a row smoothly ("baw-baw", then "baw-ah"). Learns that a letter at the end with no nekudah says its own sound: that's why Shin's "sh" and Suf's "s" matter.
- **Games:** Reading Path, Word Builder, Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** reads 2-chunk pieces smoothly.

### Unit 19. First words — 3 weeks
- **Skill:** reads short familiar words. First ideas: אַבָּא, שַׁבָּת, חַלָּה, סֻכָּה, מַצָּה, יָד, דָּג, לוּלָב. The rebbe chooses the final list.
- **Story:** the Shabbos table, Yom Tov and the brachos table.
- **Games:** Reading Path, Word Builder, a picture match (read the word, drag it to its picture), Find the Mistake.
- **Mastery:** reads the unit's words, plus new words made only from known letters and nekudos.

## Part D — Graduation

### Unit 20. Kriyah Graduation — 1 week
- A celebration review of the whole year: letters, look-alikes, finals, nekudos and words, in one happy path.
- Ends with a **certificate screen** with his name and "Mazel tov! You can read!".
