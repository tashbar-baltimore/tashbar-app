# Unit 10 (Alef-Beis Champion): builder's notes

**The request, in Esti's words:** "Build in map order ... Unit 10 (Alef-Beis Champion) ... make each new world feel new." And the variety rule.

**Checked against the map:**
- **Skill:** knows every letter's name and sound, quickly and surely, in any order. ✔
- **Games:** mixed rounds of every game kind, look-alike rounds ✔. Alef-Beis Train against a gentle clock (bonus only): **not built**, see decision 4.
- **Mastery (the gate):** names and sounds every letter in a mixed check, nearly all right (14 of 15 for 3 stars), **on two different days**. Nekudos open only after this. ✔
- **A boy who isn't there yet gets extra review rounds that focus on the letters he misses.** ✔ (My hard letters)

## The new world
**Champion Day:** a wooden stage on the green hills, an arch of balloons, all of Mendy's friends (Mendy, Shloimy, Ari, Dovid, Eli, Avi, Tzvi) cheering, and a golden gate that leads to the nekudos.

## Decisions I made on my own (each one line, so you can say yes or change it)
1. **3 stars = 14 of 15 right** on the champion check ("nearly all right").
2. **Two different days** are counted on this device (the dates he got 3 stars). When the shared sign-in saves stars on the school server, it should save these days too.
3. **My hard letters opens after the first try at the check**, even if he passed, so any boy can practise.
4. **The train against a clock is left out.** A clock can feel like pressure at 5; the bonus is the letter rain with the year's trickiest letters instead. If you want the clock train, I'll add it.
5. **The warm-up on Champion Day** asks Gimmel, Shin, Mem Sofis, Tzadik and Pei.
6. **No new letters** in Unit 10; the path shows a gold star in place of the unit's letters.
