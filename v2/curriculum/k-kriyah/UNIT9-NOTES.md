# Unit 9 (the final letters ך ם ן ף ץ): builder's notes

**The request, in Esti's words:** "Build in map order ... Unit 9 (final letters) ... make each new world feel new." And the variety rule: "no two games in a unit play the same way, and no game repeats an earlier unit's kind without a new twist."

**Checked against the map:**
- **Skill:** knows each final letter, its partner, and that it says the same sound. Tells ס from ם. ✔ (and ו from ן, ר from ך)
- **Story:** some letters wear "Shabbos clothes" at the end of a word. ✔ (told by Shloimy, see decision 1)
- **Games:** Picture Story, Partner Match, Look-Alike Detective (ס/ם, ו/ן), Find the Mistake. ✔ Plus End of the Word.
- **Mastery:** matches all 5 pairs (Partner Match) and names each final letter (the check). ✔

## The new world
**Erev Shabbos in the kitchen** (the painted kitchen picture, not used before): a warm wooden kitchen, a red pot on the stove, flowers on the table.

## Decisions I made on my own (each one line, so you can say yes or change it)
1. **Shloimy tells the story, not Chaim.** Chaim has no painted picture yet; Shloimy does.
2. **No grown-up in this story.** The Shapes art notes say parents stay off the play screens until you approve how they look.
3. **The names are "Chaf Sofis, Mem Sofis, Nun Sofis, Fei Sofis, Tzadik Sofis"** (Ashkenazi, like "Suf"). Some chadarim say "langeh Nun, shlos Mem". The rebbe can choose.
4. **Final Chaf is taught without a dot** (as it almost always is), and says "ch".
5. **End of the Word uses real words with no nekudos.** He doesn't read them: the place (the end, marked by a star) tells him. None of the words spells a Name.
6. **The review is Unit 8's Dot Builder**, quick and mixed.

## For the rebbe (every sound and rule used in Unit 9)
- **Final letters:** Chaf Sofis (ך) says "ch", Mem Sofis (ם) "m", Nun Sofis (ן) "n", Fei Sofis (ף) "f", Tzadik Sofis (ץ) "tz": the same sound as the partner.
- **Rule:** a final letter comes only at the end of a word; everywhere else the plain letter.
- **Look-alikes:** Samech is round at the bottom, Mem Sofis is square at the bottom. Vov and Reish stop at the line; Nun Sofis and Chaf Sofis have a long leg.
- **Words:** שלום, מים, ארץ, נר, מלך, צב, גן, כף (End of the Word); לחם, אבן (Find the Mistake). No nekudos are shown.
- **Temporary:** the computer voice says the words and sounds until the rebbe's recording is in.
