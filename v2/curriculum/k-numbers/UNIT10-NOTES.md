# Kindergarten Numbers, Unit 10 "Making 10" (friends of ten): notes

**What Esti asked for, in her words:** "immersive, 3D ... upgraded 2.0", and (2026-11-27) "it shouldn't be confusing ... a kid who's 5-6 years old can figure it out." Built to `v2/curriculum/K-CLARITY-RULES.md`.

**Skill (MAP.md):** for any number 1–9, knows how many more make 10 (K.OA.4). Song: "Friends of ten" (call and answer: "Seven and..." he answers "three make ten!").

## The look: a calm bakery counter, in real 3D
- `bakery10.js` (three.js): a wooden counter with a cream cloth, and the star of the unit, a **muffin tin with ten cups in two rows of five**: a real ten-frame.
- **Pink cupcakes** are the ones already there; **blue cupcakes** are the ones he adds. The two parts of ten are always two clear colours.
- A light-blue plate holds extra cupcakes (Make a Ten, Find the Mistake, Review).
- Calm and still: nothing moves unless it is part of the game.
- The bakery pictures (order N10) are not here yet: until they come, the background is Bubby's kitchen picture.

## Seven stops, seven ways to play
| Stop | How he plays | New |
|---|---|---|
| Learn it (opener10) | Count the ten cups; seven pink, three empty, three blue fill it; the song; six needs four; his turn: eight, he shows two on his fingers | friends of ten |
| Fill the tin (fill10) | He taps each empty cup (a blue cupcake drops in) until the tin is full, then shows how many he added on two hands | |
| Ten friends (pairs10) | Pink and blue number cards; he drags pink onto its blue friend; the tin proves it (too few: count the empty cups; too many: only ten cups) | matching pairs of ten |
| Make a ten (cherry10) | Eight in the tin, three on the plate; he taps the plate until the tin is full; the bond splits three into two and one; he shows what is left | the first seed of adding past ten |
| Find the mistake (mistake10) | Mendy says two numbers make ten; the tin shows the truth; he fills an empty cup, takes a left-over away, or gives a thumbs up | three kinds of fix |
| Review (review10) | Units 6–9: one less (plate), the parts of 6 and 8 (pink and blue), one more (tin), on two hands | |
| Show me (check10) | Mastery: 6, 3, 9, 5, 2 in the tin; he shows how many more make ten; the blue cupcakes prove it | |
