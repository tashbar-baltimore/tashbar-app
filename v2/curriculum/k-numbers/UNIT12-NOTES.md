## Seven stops
| Stop | How he plays |
|---|---|
| Learn it (opener12) | Six cups, three more are set: start at six and count on, seven, eight, nine. Nine candies, four eaten: five left (a full row). The song. His turn: seven and two more, on two hands |
| Story builder (story12) | A Shabbos story; he acts it out (taps the tray, or the candies), then drags in the sign and the answer (cards 1–10) |
| Hop on! (hop12) | "Six plus three": he puts Shmuli on six, then taps each next stone, counting on (minus: he hops back) |
| Ten-frame add (tenadd12) | The pink cupcakes are in the tin; he taps the plate and the blue ones jump in; he sees the total (a full row is five, one empty cup is nine) and shows it on two hands |
| Find the mistake (mistake12) | Shmuli hops a fact wrong (one too many, or counting his own stone); the boy moves him to the right stone, and Shmuli hops it again, counting on |
| Review (review12) | Units 9–11: seven is five and how many; how many more make ten; a fact within 5 |
| Show me (check12) | Mastery (MAP): 6 mixed stories within 10, each acted out with its number sentence |
