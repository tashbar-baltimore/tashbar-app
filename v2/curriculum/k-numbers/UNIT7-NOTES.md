## The look: a real 3D apple orchard
- `orchard7.js` (three.js): the painted orchard panorama far behind (order N7), painted orchard grass, a big apple tree with apples, orchard trees around.
- Two picnic blankets (painted blue-and-cream checks) with glossy 3D apples (painted apple skin). Dovid and Shmuel (the painted friends from Shapes) stand beside their blankets.
- Pairing: partners line up in columns facing each other, joined by a golden ribbon; the apples left over show who has more.
- An apple drops from the tree in an arc (one more); one rolls back (one less). A wooden see-saw (painted plank) tips toward the side with more and levels when the same.
- Phones and Chromebooks: the blankets are one behind the other, so partners line up straight. Every tap spot is at least 64 px, and apples sit 0.92 apart so their spots never overlap.

## Eight stops, eight different ways to play
| Stop | How he plays | New to the Numbers year |
|---|---|---|
| Learn it (opener7) | Line up shows more and less; four and four is the same; the song (stand up); one more and one less from the tree; he taps who has more | |
| Line them up (pairs7) | Tap one apple of each friend: they become partners. Leftovers show who has more, or "the same" | one-to-one pairing |
| Which is more? (more7) | Only numerals on cards; he taps the bigger number; then the apples come down in two lines to check | comparing written numbers |
| One more, one less (build7) | "Give Shmuel one more (one less, the same) than Dovid": tap the tree, tap an apple to put it back, check | building one more / one less |
| The see-saw (seesaw7) | More goes down. Add apples to the high side (or take one from the low side) until it is level | balance as "the same" |
| Find the mistake (mistake7) | Ari's three mistakes: spread out is not more; a wrong "one more"; "four is more than six" | the spread-out trick |
| Review (review7) | Units 4–6: a written number 0–10, he picks that many apples (rows of five: five and some more) | |
| Show me (check7) | Mastery: groups (scattered, and the same), numbers, one more and one less | |
