## Seven stops, seven ways to play
| Stop | How he plays | New to the Numbers year |
|---|---|---|
| Learn it (opener8) | Counters spill: three and two make five; two plates: four and one; the chant (stand up); the towel hides one; he shows how many hide | |
| How many hiding? (hide8) | Count the kugel slices; a towel drops over some; he shows the hidden part on his fingers; the towel lifts to check | the hidden part |
| Shake and spill (shake8) | He taps the cup: it shakes and spills; he shows the red part on the red hand and the yellow part on the yellow hand | two parts at once |
| Bond builder (bond8) | A number bond (whole on top, two parts below) with one empty circle; he drags the right number in; the plates prove it | part, part, whole |
| Find the mistake (mistake8) | Ari's bond has one wrong number; the plates show the truth; he finds the wrong circle and fixes it | finding which number is wrong |
| Review (review8) | Units 5–7: which plate has more (or the same); a ten-frame on two hands; one more than six on two hands | |
| Show me (check8) | Mastery: how many hiding for 3, 4 and 5, then two number bonds | |
