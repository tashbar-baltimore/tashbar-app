# Kindergarten Numbers — the full-year map (Tashbar Learning 2.0)

**The goal:** fun, easy, skill-building games that teach every kind of boy real skills, one step on top of the next, all year, until he graduates.

**The year:** 14 units and a graduation, about 35 school weeks. Each day takes 10–15 minutes, and each game takes 2–4 minutes.

**Research behind it** (approved summary): learning paths (Clements & Sarama), the counting rules (Gelman & Gallistel), seeing at a glance (Clements), straight-path board games (Siegler & Ramani), real things before pictures before numbers (Bruner, Singapore), number bonds and "how many are hiding" (Richardson, Math Recovery), acted-out story problems (Carpenter), "ten and some more" (Miura), finger tracing (Montessori), and spaced, mixed review (Dunlosky).

---

## How every game works (the same in every unit)

- **Show, then try.** A short demo with voice: "Watch me… now you!" A big **hear it again** button is always on screen. Spoken lines are 12 words or fewer, one instruction at a time, and there is no reading.
- **Real things, then pictures, then numbers.** Every unit starts with things he can drag and move. Then come dots and frames, and the written number last.
- **Stars.** A game gives up to 3 stars. 2 stars opens the next game.
- **Too hard?** If he misses twice, the game quietly gets easier: fewer things, a picture hint, or the voice counts with him.
- **Too easy?** If he is quick and right, he gets a **bonus round** with bigger numbers or a hidden part.
- **Mistakes teach.** A wrong answer is never just "wrong". The game shows why ("Let's count together… 1, 2, 3, 4. Four!") and lets him try again. Every unit has one **find the mistake** game.
- **Celebrations** last 2 seconds, then it's back to learning.

**Every day:**
1. **Warm-up chant** (1 minute). Counting out loud, growing all year: to 10, then 20, then by tens to 100, then by ones to 100.
2. **Today's game(s).**
3. **Mixed review round** (1–2 minutes). Short items from earlier units, spaced out: yesterday's skill, one from a few weeks ago, and one older.

**Before Unit 1 (built once): the frame.** The boy signs in with his code. He sees his path of games in order, full screen, with nothing else on screen.

## Unit 1. How many? Look and say (1–3, then 1–5) — 2 weeks

**Skill:** sees small amounts at a glance, without counting.
**Standard:** groundwork for K.CC.4 and K.CC.5.
**Chant:** a "Peek and see!" clapping chant.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Peek! | Apples flash for one second. He puts the same number in the basket. | Quick flash, so he must *see*, not count. 1–3 first, then 4–5. |
| Same amount | He drags dice, fingers and Shabbos candles that show the same amount together. | The same number looks different but is still the same. |
| Finger flash | A hand flashes. He raises the same fingers on a big hand picture. | Finger patterns as a built-in tool. |
| Find the mistake | "Shloimy says there are 3. Is he right?" He fixes the basket. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** 8 quick flashes of 1–5 in mixed layouts. He builds each one; 7 of 8 right.
**Slow help:** stay at 1–3 longer, with longer flashes. **Bonus:** 5 seen as "3 and 2".

## Unit 2. Counting out loud to 10 — 1 week (then the daily chant all year)

**Skill:** says the number words in order: 1–10, from any number, and back from 10.
**Standard:** K.CC.1, K.CC.2.
**Song:** a counting song with jumps and claps.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Count and jump | Stand up! He jumps and claps along as the voice counts to 10. | Movement plus rhythm to learn the word list. |
| What comes next? | The voice counts and stops. He taps the next stepping stone. | Learning the order, starting from any number. |
| Count down | Countdown to Tatty's car leaving for shul: 10…1. | Counting backward. |
| Find the mistake | Mendy counts "1, 2, 3, 5". He taps where Mendy skipped. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** he finishes counts that start at 1, 4 and 7, and one countdown from 10.
**Slow help:** stop at 5 first. **Bonus:** count to 20.

## Unit 3. Counting things: touch each once, and the last number tells how many — 2 weeks

**Skill:** counts things one by one, says how many, and can "give me 4".
**Standard:** K.CC.4a, K.CC.4b, K.CC.5.
**Chant:** "Touch it, say it, move it away."

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Touch and count | He taps each siddur. It hops onto the shelf as the voice counts. Then: "How many?" | One touch, one number. The last number is the answer. |
| Give me! | "Bubby needs 4 challah rolls." He drags exactly 4 onto the plate and taps done. | "Give me a number" is the real test of understanding. |
| Mix it up | Count, then the things get scattered. "How many now?" | Order doesn't matter. |
| Find the mistake | Ari counts one apple twice. He taps the apple Ari counted twice. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** "Give me" 3, 5, 7 and 9, plus counting a scattered group of up to 10.
**Slow help:** things in a line, up to 5. **Bonus:** scattered groups up to 12.

## Unit 4. Numbers 0–5: see it, say it, show it, find it, trace it — 3 weeks

**Skill:** knows 0–5 as a group, a finger pattern, a five-frame and a written number.
**Standard:** K.CC.3 (0–5), K.CC.4.
**Song:** a short tracing rhyme for each number.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Five-frame fill | He fills a five-frame with the number he hears. | Seeing a number as "boxes filled". |
| Match it up | He matches a group to fingers to the written number. | Real things, then pictures, then numbers. |
| Trace it | He traces a big 0–5 with his finger while saying its name. | Montessori finger tracing. |
| Empty plate (zero) | "How many cookies are left?" None. Zero! | Zero as an empty group. |
| Find the mistake | Dovid put 4 dots for "3". He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** for each of 0–5: build it, show it on fingers, and drag the written number to its group.
**Review:** Units 1–3.

## Unit 5. Numbers 6–10: five and some more — 3 weeks

**Skill:** sees 6–10 as "5 and some more", on a ten-frame, on two hands and on a bead rack.
**Standard:** K.CC.3 (6–10), K.CC.5.
**Chant:** "Five and one is six…"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Ten-frame fill | He fills a ten-frame: 7 is a full row and 2 more. | The five-and-ten structure. |
| Bead push | "Push 8." He slides 5 red and 3 white beads in one move. | Bead rack (the Dutch method): don't count by ones. |
| Two hands | He raises fingers on two big hands to match a number. | Fingers as fives. |
| Trace it | He traces 6–10. | Finger tracing. |
| Find the mistake | Eli says this ten-frame shows 9, but it shows 8. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** flash ten-frames 6–10, then build 6–10 on frames and beads.
**Review:** Units 1–4.

## Unit 6. The path game 1–10 (number line) — 2 weeks

**Skill:** knows where numbers live on a line in order. One step forward is one more.
**Standard:** K.CC.2, K.CC.4c.
**Chant:** say each number as you step.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Path race | He spins 1 or 2 and moves his piece along a straight 1–10 path, saying each number. | Siegler & Ramani's straight-path game. |
| Where does it go? | He drags written numbers onto their spots on the path. | The "number line in your head". |
| Big steps | Stand up! "Take 3 steps forward. Where are you?" | Walking the number line. |
| Find the mistake | Avi's piece landed on the wrong spot. He moves it right. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** he places 5 numbers on an empty 0–10 path, and plays one full race correctly.
**Review:** Units 3–5.

## Unit 7. More, less, the same, one more, one less — 3 weeks

**Skill:** compares two groups and two numbers. Tells one more and one less.
**Standard:** K.CC.4c, K.CC.6, K.CC.7.
**Song:** "One more, one more, step up one more…"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Line them up | He pairs Tzvi's and Moshe's apples one to one. Who has more? | Matching pairs shows "more" without counting. |
| Which is more? | He taps the bigger number, then checks on the path. The later number is more. | Compare on the number line. |
| One more, one less | "Add one more apple. How many now?" He builds it. | Each number is one more than the one before. |
| Find the mistake | Chaim says 4 is more than 6. He shows why not. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** compare groups up to 10 (scattered too), compare numbers 1–10, one more and one less.
**Review:** Units 4–6.

## Unit 8. Parts that make a number (up to 5) — 2 weeks

**Skill:** knows the two parts that make 2, 3, 4 and 5.
**Standard:** K.OA.3.
**Chant:** "Four is three and one…"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Shake and spill | He shakes a cup of 5 two-color counters, spills them, and says the parts. | Number bonds (Singapore). |
| How many are hiding? | 5 kugel slices, a cover goes over some. How many are under the cover? | The hidden part (Richardson, Math Recovery). |
| Bond builder | He drags parts into a number-bond picture. | Part, part, whole. |
| Find the mistake | "3 and 1 makes 5." He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** "how many are hiding" for 3, 4 and 5, then fill two number bonds.
**Review:** Units 5–7.

## Unit 9. Parts that make 6 to 10 — 2 weeks

**Skill:** knows the parts of 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
**Standard:** K.OA.3.
**Chant:** a call-and-answer: "Seven is…" "Five and two!"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Shake and spill to 10 | Same game, bigger numbers. | Number bonds. |
| Under the cup | Up to 10 things, some hidden. How many are hiding? | The hidden part. |
| Split it | He splits a ten-frame two ways with a slider. | Seeing all the ways. |
| Find the mistake | Yaakov's bond for 8 is wrong. He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** find the hidden part for 6–10, and show 2 ways to make 7.
**Review:** Units 6–8.

## Unit 10. Making 10 (friends of 10) — 2 weeks

**Skill:** for any number 1–9, knows how many more make 10.
**Standard:** K.OA.4.
**Song:** "Friends of 10".

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Fill the ten | A ten-frame has 6. He adds the counters it needs. | Ten-frame partners. |
| Ten pairs | He matches pairs that make 10 (7 with 3). | Friends of 10. |
| Cherry split | He splits a number to finish a ten. | Japan's "cherry" method, the first seed of adding past 10. |
| Find the mistake | Mendy says 6 and 3 make 10. He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** the partner of 10 for 8 numbers, with the ten-frame hidden for the last 4.
**Review:** Units 7–9.

## Unit 11. Adding and taking away within 5 — 3 weeks

**Skill:** solves "join" and "take away" stories within 5, then reads + − =, and knows these quickly.
**Standard:** K.OA.1, K.OA.2, K.OA.5.
**Chant:** "Put together — plus! Take away — minus!"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Act it out | "3 boys in the sukkah, 1 more comes." He drags the boys in and says the total. | Story first, act it out (Carpenter). |
| Take away | "4 cookies, Ari eats 2." He drags 2 away. How many are left? | Act it out. |
| Story to signs | The same stories now get the number sentence 3 + 1 = 4 built underneath. | Real things, then pictures, then numbers. |
| Quick facts | Short rounds of facts within 5, with a picture help button. | Recall to build speed. |
| Find the mistake | Dovid says 2 + 2 = 5. He shows why not. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** 4 story problems (act it out), plus 10 facts within 5. 8 of 10 right without the help button.
**Review:** Units 8–10.

## Unit 12. Adding and taking away within 10 — 3 weeks

**Skill:** solves add and take-away stories within 10, and writes the number sentence.
**Standard:** K.OA.1, K.OA.2.
**Song:** a Shabbos table story song.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Story builder | Shabbos table stories within 10. He acts each one out, then builds the sentence. | Act it out, then numbers. |
| Hop on the path | 6 + 3: start at 6 and hop 3. | Counting on along the number line. |
| Ten-frame add | He adds on the ten-frame and sees the total without counting by ones. | Five-and-ten structure. |
| Find the mistake | Shloimy hopped wrong on the path. He fixes the hops. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** 6 mixed story problems within 10 (add and take away), each with its number sentence.
**Review:** Units 9–11.

## Unit 13. Teen numbers: ten and some more (11–20) — 3 weeks

**Skill:** sees 11–19 as ten and some ones. Counts, writes and places numbers to 20.
**Standard:** K.NBT.1, K.CC.3 (to 20), K.CC.5 (to 20).
**Chant:** "Ten and three, thirteen!"

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Ten and some more | A full ten-frame plus extras. He builds 14 as "ten and four". | "Ten and some more", the way Chinese and Japanese say it. |
| Bundle it | He bundles 10 sticks, then adds loose ones. | Ten ones make one ten. |
| Path race to 20 | The board game again, now 1–20. | Straight-path game. |
| Trace it | He traces 11–20. | Finger tracing. |
| Find the mistake | Eli built 16 as ten and 5. He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** build 5 teen numbers, then match ten-frames to written numbers 11–20.
**Review:** Units 10–12.

## Unit 14. Counting to 100 by ones and by tens — 3 weeks

**Skill:** counts to 100 by ones and by tens, and counts on from any number.
**Standard:** K.CC.1, K.CC.2.
**Song:** a counting-by-tens song with jumps.

| Game | What he does | The trick |
|---|---|---|
| Tens train | He counts 10 at a time as train cars of 10 roll in. | Skip-counting. |
| Hundred chart path | He fills missing numbers on a 100 chart and sees the patterns down each column. | Pattern and structure. |
| Count on from here | "Start at 47, count 5 more." He hops along. | Counting on from any number. |
| Find the mistake | Avi counts "…28, 29, 20". He fixes it. | Mistakes teach. |

**Mastery check:** count by tens to 100, fill 5 holes in the chart, and count on from 3 starting numbers.
**Review:** Units 11–13.

## Graduation — 1 week

A celebration review of the whole year. One short round from every unit, in skill order, then mixed.
- He finishes the year's chant, all the way to 100.
- A certificate screen with his name, which the boy sees, and a short spoken mazel tov. Nothing else.

## What I changed from the starting skeleton, and why

1. **Counting out loud** is still Unit 2, but it is also a daily 1-minute chant all year, growing to 100. Saying the number list is learned by daily repetition, not in one unit.
2. **Writing numbers** is no longer its own unit. Each number is traced when it is learned (Units 4, 5 and 13). Tracing works best tied to knowing the number. Writing now goes to 20, because the standard asks for 0–20.
3. **The path game** moved earlier, right after numbers 0–10, and comes back for 1–20 in Unit 13. Siegler's study used a 1–10 path, and the path helps comparing (Unit 7) and adding (Unit 12).
4. **Zero** got its own game in Unit 4.
5. **Parts of a number** are split into two units: up to 5, then 6–10.
6. **Number of units:** 14 units plus graduation.
