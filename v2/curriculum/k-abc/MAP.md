# Kindergarten ABC — the full-year map (Esti: "the map looks fine", 2026-11-23)

**The goal:** fun, easy, skill-building games that teach every kind of boy real skills, one step on top of the next, all year, until he graduates.

**Esti's decisions (2026-11-23):**
- Built fully new for 2.0. Nothing is copied from 1.0.
- Sounds start in week 2, alongside the listening games.
- Blending starts as soon as he knows s, a, t.
- Writing each letter happens on the day the letter is taught.
- **Print basics are things a boy does, never things he reads.** Nothing boring.
- **The look (later the same day): cute, cartoony, immersive 3D, "nothing simple".** Every game happens in one 3D world (the meadow kit, `v2/app/k-abc/world.js`).
- **Each letter turns into its picture as it is traced.** For example, the snake grows along the s while the computer traces it, and again under the boy's finger.

## Rules for the whole year
- **Sound order** (research-based, not A to Z): s a t p · i n m d · g o c k ck e · u r h b f l ff ll ss · j v w x y z zz qu · then sh ch th.
- **Pure sounds:** "sss", never "suh".
- **No reading needed for instructions.** Every instruction is spoken, with a big "hear it again" button.
- **Only known sounds:** every word he is asked to read uses sounds he already has, or a taught heart word.
- **Boys' names** (Mendy, Ari, Dovid, Eli, Tzvi…) always come with the boy's picture, so a name never stops him reading.
- **A day** is 10–15 minutes: 3 or 4 games of 2–4 minutes each.
- **Moving on:** the next game opens at 2 stars out of 3. If he struggles, the game quietly gives more help and easier steps.
- **Bonus round:** quick boys get a harder round.
- **Review:** every unit ends with a short mixed review of earlier units. Big review weeks come at weeks 8, 18 and 28.
- **Every unit has:**
  - an opener (a short, warm lesson)
  - a short chant or song
  - one "find the mistake" game
  - a mastery check

## The letter kit: the same 5 games for every new letter (used in units 2–6)
Boys learn the game once, then spend their time on the letter.
1. **Meet the letter (the opener):**
   - A picture turns into its letter (a snake curls into *s*).
   - He hears the pure sound and sees a mouth picture of how to say it.
   - He copies a body action (a snake wiggle for "sss").
   - Methods: picture inside the letter; see it, say it, hear it.
2. **Trace the path:**
   - He traces with his finger along arrows while a short rhyme plays.
   - Three rounds: on the picture, then on dots, then alone.
   - Methods: writing by hand; moving and touching.
3. **Sound hunt:**
   - He drags pictures that start with the new sound into a basket. The others go back on the shelf.
   - Method: listening for the first sound.
4. **Slide it:**
   - Letter tiles sit on a slide. He pushes them together, left to right: *s–a–t … sat!*
   - Then he drags the word onto its picture.
   - Methods: blending; left to right.
5. **Fix it (find the mistake):**
   - "Ari read this as *sit*. Did he read it right?"
   - He gives a thumbs up or thumbs down, then drags in the right tile.
   - Method: mistakes teach.

**Mastery check (tap kinds, all spoken):**
- hear a sound, tap its letter
- see a letter, say or tap its sound
- blend 3 words

## Unit 1 — Ears On (week 1)
- **Skill:** hear rhymes, clap word parts, and hear the first sound of a word. Learn that we read left to right. No letters yet.
- **Standards:** RF.K.2a, 2b, 2c (rhyme, syllables, first sound); RF.K.1a (left to right).
- **Games:**
  - **Rhyme pairs:** he drags *cat* to *hat* and *sun* to *bun*. Method: rhyme, with pictures and voice.
  - **Clap it:** he taps a drum once for each word part ("Shab-bos" = 2 claps). Method: rhythm and moving.
  - **Who starts with mmm?:** he sorts pictures into two baskets by their first sound. Method: first-sound listening.
  - **The train goes this way:** he pushes picture-cars onto a track that only runs left to right, then "reads" the pictures in order. Method: print basics done as play.
  - **Fix it:** "Eli says *sun* and *map* rhyme. Is he right?"
- **Chant:** a clapping rhyme about getting ready for cheder.
- **Mastery check:**
  - tap the picture that rhymes
  - tap how many claps
  - tap the picture that starts with a sound
- **Listening keeps going:** these games return as 1-minute warm-ups all year.

## Unit 2 — First sounds: s a t p (weeks 2–4)
- **Skill:** know the sounds s, a, t, p, write each one, and blend them into words.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a (sounds of consonants); RF.K.3b (short a); RF.K.2d (blending); RF.K.1 (left to right).
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for each letter
  - **Slide it** starts after *t*, with *at* and *sat*
- **Words:** at, sat, pat, tap, sap.
- **Story moment:** a 3-picture story read aloud to him: "Tatty sat. Pat, pat, pat!" He finds the words he can read.
- **Chant:** the s-a-t-p action chant (wiggle, apple-chomp, tick-tock, pop).
- **Mastery check:**
  - the letter kit check
  - blend at, sat, tap

## Unit 3 — i n m d (weeks 5–7)
- **Skill:** four new sounds, each used in words the same day. Hear 2–3 sounds in a short word.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a, 3b; RF.K.2d.
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for each letter
  - **Sound boxes (light):** he hears a picture word and pushes one token into a box for each sound (*it* = 2, *pin* = 3). Method: Elkonin boxes.
- **Words:** it, in, sit, pin, tin, nap, man, mat, map, dip, dad, did, mad.
- **Chant:** "Mmm, the Shabbos soup is yum."
- **Mastery check:**
  - letter kit check
  - blend 4 words
  - box the sounds in 2 words

## Review week A (week 8)
- **What happens:** mixed, short rounds of everything so far: sounds, tracing, blending, rhyme.
- **Help:** a boy who missed a sound gets that letter's kit again, quietly.
- **Quick boys:** a "speed slide" bonus round.

## Unit 4 — g o c k ck e (weeks 9–11)
- **Skill:** five sounds, including that *c*, *k* and *ck* all say "k", and *ck* goes at the end.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a, 3b (short o, e).
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for each
  - **k-sound sort:** he drags words into the right house: *c* house, *k* house, *ck* house (end of word). Method: sorting by pattern.
- **Words:** got, dog, pot, top, cot, cap, kid, kit, peg, pen, net, ten, sock, neck, pick.
- **Chant:** "Kick, kick, ck at the end."
- **Mastery check:**
  - letter kit check
  - blend 5 words
  - one k-sound sort

## Unit 5 — u r h b f l, with ff ll ss (weeks 12–14)
- **Skill:** six sounds, plus "double letters at the end make one sound".
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a, 3b (short u).
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for each
  - **b or d? (Fix it):** the bat comes first, then the ball (*b*); the drum comes first, then the stick (*d*). He fixes flipped letters. Method: picture memory trick.
- **Words:** sun, cup, run, bus, hat, bed, fan, fig, log, leg, hug, bun, hill, bell, doll, puff, off, mess.
- **Chant:** "Bubby's bell goes b-b-b."
- **Mastery check:**
  - letter kit check
  - blend 5 words
  - b/d fix

## Unit 6 — j v w x y z zz qu (weeks 15–17)
- **Skill:** the last sounds. Every sound is done.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a.
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for each (*qu* is taught as one sound, "kw")
  - **Sound train:** all letters ride in order of when he learned them, and he says each sound as it passes.
- **Words:** jam, jet, van, vet, web, wet, wig, box, fox, six, mix, yes, yet, zip, buzz, quit, quack.
- **Chant:** the whole-year sound chant, all actions.
- **Mastery check:**
  - say or tap all sounds (mixed order)
  - blend 5 words

## Review week B (week 18, mid-year)
- **What happens:** a celebration review of all sounds and blending.
- **Help:** each boy's weak sounds come back in short extra rounds.
- **Quick boys:** a bonus "build the longest word you can" round.

## Unit 7 — Letter names, the ABC song, Tatty letters and small letters (weeks 19–20)
- **Skill:** know each letter's name. Match every capital letter to its small letter.
- **Standards:** RF.K.1d (name all capital and small letters).
- **Games:**
  - **The ABC song:** it lights up each letter as it is sung.
  - **Find your Tatty letter:** each small letter runs to its big capital "Tatty letter" (*b* runs to *B*). Method: print basics done as play.
  - **Trace the capitals:** the trace path, capitals only.
  - **Fix it:** "Dovid put small *d* with big *B*. Is that right?"
- **Note:** the sound always comes first: "This is *b*. It says b-b-b."
- **Mastery check:**
  - hear a name, tap the letter
  - match 6 capital and small pairs

## Unit 8 — Short-vowel word power (weeks 21–22)
- **Skill:** read and build 3-sound words with all five short vowels mixed.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3b (short vowels); RF.K.2d.
- **Games:**
  - **Slide it (mixed):** blending with a, e, i, o, u.
  - **Build it:** he hears "pin", then drags letter tiles into 3 slots.
  - **Change one letter:** *cat → hat → hot → hop*. He swaps one tile at a time.
  - **Fix it:** "Mendy built *pan* for *pen*. Fix it!"
- **Mastery check:**
  - read 6 mixed words
  - build 3 words

## Unit 9 — Every sound in a word (weeks 23–24)
- **Skill:** hear and tap every sound in a word, and spell it with tiles.
- **Standards:** RF.K.2d (isolate and say every sound in 3-sound words); RF.K.2e (change one sound to make a new word).
- **Games:**
  - **Tap a dot:** he hears a word and taps one dot per sound.
  - **Sound boxes (full):** he pushes tokens, then puts letters in the boxes.
  - **Middle sound:** he drags the picture to the vowel in its middle (*bus* to *u*).
  - **Fix it:** "Eli tapped 2 dots for *sun*. How many should it be?"
- **Mastery check:**
  - tap the sounds in 4 words
  - spell 3 words

## Unit 10 — Heart words (weeks 25–27)
- **Skill:** read common words that can't all be sounded out, a few at a time.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3c (read common high-frequency words by sight).
- **Words (in this order):**
  - I, the, a, is, to, he, we, my, said, was, of, you, are
  - family words: Tatty, Mommy, Bubby, Zeidy
- **Games:**
  - **Heart it:** he sounds out the regular parts. The tricky part gets a red heart, and he traces it.
  - **Heart word hunt:** he drags the heart word out of a pile.
  - **Flash and catch:** a quick retrieval game with words from earlier weeks mixed in.
  - **Fix it:** "Ari read *the* as *t-h-e*. What do we say?"
- **Mastery check:** read all heart words taught so far, mixed.

## Review week C (week 28)
- **What happens:** mixed review of sounds, letter names, word building, sounds in words and heart words.
- **Help:** extra rounds go to each boy's weak spots.

## Unit 11 — My first sentences (weeks 29–30)
- **Skill:** read short sentences made only of known sounds and heart words, and know what they mean.
- **Standards:** RF.K.4 (read simple text with understanding); RF.K.1b, 1c (words are separated by spaces; spoken words match written words).
- **Games:**
  - **Word hop:** he hears a sentence and hops a frog from stone to stone, one stone per word. Method: spaces between words, done as play.
  - **Read and match:** he reads "The cat is on the mat." and drags it to the right picture.
  - **Build a sentence:** he drags word cards into order after hearing the sentence.
  - **Fix it:** "Moshe read 'The dog is in the bed.' The picture shows a box. Is it right?"
- **Mastery check:**
  - read 3 sentences
  - match each to its picture

## Unit 12 — sh ch th, and -s for more than one (weeks 31–33)
- **Skill:** two letters that make one sound, and adding -s for more than one.
- **Standards:** RF.K.3a; RF.K.3d (spot the different letter in similar words).
- **Games:**
  - the letter kit for sh, ch, th (with pictures: a finger on the lips for *sh*, a train going *ch-ch*)
  - **One or many?:** *cat → cats*. He drags an -s onto the word when there is more than one.
- **Words:** ship, shop, fish, dish, chin, chip, chop, much, thin, bath, path, moth, with, cats, hats, pens, cups.
- **Chant:** "Sh, sh, the baby's asleep."
- **Mastery check:**
  - read 6 words with sh, ch, th
  - read 3 words with -s

## Unit 13 — Little stories from home (weeks 34–35)
- **Skill:** read a short story (6–8 pages) out loud, with help on tap.
- **Standards:** RF.K.4; RF.K.1 (how a book works).
- **Stories (all decodable, warm frum family life):**
  - "Shabbos Fish"
  - "The Sukkah Hut"
  - "Bubby's Big Hug"
  - "Mendy and the Lost Hat"
- **How it works:**
  - He reads each page aloud.
  - He can tap a word to hear it, slowly.
  - He taps the arrow to turn the page. Method: how a book works, done as play.
- **After each story:** a question in pictures ("What did Tatty find?"); he drags the answer picture.
- **Find the mistake:** one page read wrong by "Chaim". He spots it.
- **Mastery check:** read one new story with no more than 3 help-taps per page.

## Unit 14 — Graduation (week 36)
- **What happens:** a celebration review. One quick round from each unit, in order, like walking back along the whole year's path.
- **Then:** a certificate screen with his name, "Kindergarten ABC — I can read!", and a short happy chant.
- **Help:** a boy who needs more time on a skill gets those rounds first, then graduates.

---

## How we know the year worked
By graduation he can:
- hear rhymes, word parts and every sound in a short word
- say the sound and the name of every letter, small and capital
- write every letter along its path
- read short-vowel words and words with sh, ch, th and -s
- read the year's heart words
- read a short story out loud and know what it said
