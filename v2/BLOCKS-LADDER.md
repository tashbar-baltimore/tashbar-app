# Block Builders World: the ladder (DRAFT, waiting for the approver's decisions)

**What we are making (approver's words):** "Take our Block Builders workbook and build it up and down the skill levels, with the same blocks and similar concepts. Be very ambitious and very creative. The graphics should be extraordinary."

**How the ladder works**
- One set of blocks from K to 8. Each grade has its own **mat** (the work board the boy builds on). Each mat grows out of the one before it.
- Every skill moves from real blocks, to pictures, to numbers only. When a boy struggles, he goes back a step and gets the blocks back. When he does well, the blocks fade away.
- Every unit opens with **Watch Me** (a block friend teaches the example), then hands-on building, then a **Builder Detective** (find and fix the mistake).
- Every grade he finishes adds a building to his **Block Town** (no money, no store).
- **The block friends:** Dot (a one) and Tenzi (a ten) are in the workbook already. The new friends need names (the approver decides): the hundred flat, the thousand cube, the minus tile, the x tile and the x² tile.

Statuses: **Planned** · **Mat drawn** · **Building** · **In 2.0**
