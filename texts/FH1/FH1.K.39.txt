A fox sat in a pit. The fox can see six hens.
===
The fox said, "Yum! Hens!" The fox ran to the hens.
===
The hens ran to Dov. Dov had a big net. Dov said, "Go, fox!"

The fox ran to its den. The hens sat in the sun.
