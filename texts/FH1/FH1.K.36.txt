Dan said to Gad, "You and me can dig." Dan and Gad dig and dig.
===
Dan gets a pot. Gad gets a lid. "Is it a gem?" said Gad.
===
It was not a gem. It was a bun! The pups hid it in the mud.

Dan and Gad had fun.
