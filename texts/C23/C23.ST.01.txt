title: The Noise in the Sukkah
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: The Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
On the fourth night of Sukkos, Dovid Katz could not fall asleep. He was sleeping in the sukkah with his big brother, Ari. The stars peeked through the schach. The air was cool and smelled like pine.

Then Dovid heard a noise. Scratch, scratch, scratch.

"Ari," he whispered. "Did you hear that?"

Ari was snoring.

Scratch, scratch. The noise came from the corner, near the table. Dovid pulled his blanket up to his nose. He was scared, but he was also brave. He reached for his flashlight and clicked it on.
===
Two shiny eyes looked back at him. It was a little gray squirrel! It was sitting on the table, holding a piece of challah from dinner.

Dovid laughed so loud that Ari woke up.

"What is it?" Ari asked, sitting up fast.

"A guest," said Dovid. "He came for the challah."

The squirrel ran up the wall and out through the schach. Dovid and Ari put the rest of the challah in a box with a tight lid.

In the morning, Dovid told the whole Shabbos Street Club. "We had a guest in our sukkah," he said. "A very small guest with a very big tail."
--- words
schach: the branches or mats that make the roof of a sukkah
peeked: looked quickly through a small space
guest: someone who visits
