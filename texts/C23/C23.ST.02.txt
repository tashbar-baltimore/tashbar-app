title: Why Chaim Was Late
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: The Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
Every Tuesday, the Shabbos Street Club met at four o'clock. Every Tuesday, Chaim Weiss came first. But this Tuesday, four o'clock came and went. Chaim did not come.

"Where is Chaim?" asked Naftali. "He is never late."

"Maybe he forgot," said Dovid.

"Chaim never forgets anything," said Avi.

At four thirty, Chaim ran into the garage. His shoes were muddy. His face was red. He was holding a small bird's nest in his hands.
===
"I am sorry I am late," Chaim said. "On the way here, I saw this nest on the sidewalk. The wind blew it out of the tree. There are two eggs inside."

The boys looked. Two tiny blue eggs sat in the nest.

"How did you put it back?" asked Naftali.

"I could not reach the branch," said Chaim. "So I knocked on Mr. Klein's door. He brought his ladder. I held the ladder, and he put the nest back high in the tree."

"That is why your shoes are muddy," said Avi.

"And that is why you are late," said Dovid.

Chaim smiled. "Yes," he said. "Being late was worth it. Now the eggs are safe."
--- words
muddy: covered with wet dirt
nest: a home that a bird builds out of sticks and grass
ladder: steps you climb to reach something high
