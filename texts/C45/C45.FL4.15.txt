Dear Zeidy,

Greetings from Camp Pine Lake! I am writing this on my top bunk with a flashlight, because it is rest hour and my counselor says rest hour is for resting. Writing is resting, I told him. He laughed and said fine.

First, the good news. I passed my swimming test on the first try! I had to swim two lengths of the lake dock and float on my back for one whole minute. The water was freezing, but I did not complain, because you told me that a strong person does not complain about cold water. Well, I complained a little, but only inside my head.

Second, the exciting news. Tomorrow morning we go on our first real hike, all the way up Ridge Trail! Reb Akiva, our counselor, made us learn the four rules of the trail by heart. Stay together. Stay on the trail. Drink water before you are thirsty. And if you are lost, stop, sit by a big tree and blow your whistle three times. I said them to myself four times before Mincha, and I drew a map of the trail in my notebook from the sign at the bottom.
===
Tzvi Hirsch has already laced his sneakers twice, and it is still today. He says we will set a camp record. I said a camp record for what? He said he will decide tomorrow.

Third, the bad news. The food is not like Bubby's. On Shabbos we had chicken, and it was fine, but it was not Bubby's chicken. Please tell her I said so. She will like that.

I miss you. I miss learning with you on Sunday mornings. I am trying to learn a little every night with my chavrusa, Binyamin. We are learning the same mishna you taught me, and I am the one who explains it! It is harder to explain than to listen.

Write back soon. And please send more of Bubby's mandelbroit. A lot more.

Your grandson,
Avrumi
--- words
greetings: a friendly hello
complain: to say that you are unhappy about something
chavrusa: a learning partner
mishna: a part of the Oral Torah that boys learn by heart and explain
