id: C45.FL5.07
title: Fast and Slow (a poem for two voices)
grade: 5
kind: play
topic: Glen Avenue Stories
source: original
---
TZVI HIRSCH: I'm fast!

YEHUDA: I'm slow.

TZVI HIRSCH: I run ahead.

YEHUDA: I stop to look.

TZVI HIRSCH: I shout the answer.

YEHUDA: I check the book.

TZVI HIRSCH: I jump right in, I don't think twice!

YEHUDA: I think, and think, and think it thrice.

TZVI HIRSCH: He drives me crazy, slow as snow.

YEHUDA: He drives me crazy, go, go, go!
===
TZVI HIRSCH: But when the esrog box was bare,

YEHUDA: and when he blamed the new boy there,

TZVI HIRSCH: it was his notebook, page by page,

YEHUDA: that cooled his temper, calmed his rage.

TZVI HIRSCH: And when we found the leaves that day,

YEHUDA: and I stood thinking, in the way,

TZVI HIRSCH: I shouted, "Up! Before it's late!"

YEHUDA: And up we climbed. He would not wait.

TZVI HIRSCH: So now I wait a little bit.

YEHUDA: And now I run, a little bit.

BOTH: Fast and slow, and slow and fast:
we each need the other, first to last.
--- words
thrice: three times
temper: how quickly a person gets angry
rage: very strong anger
