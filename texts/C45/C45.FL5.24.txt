title: The Last Move
grade: 5
kind: poem
topic: Glen Avenue Stories
source: original
pic: chess-pieces-on-board.webp

The room is quiet. Not a sound.
The clock ticks on. The boys crowd round.
Across the board, my rival waits,
his king behind his castle gates.

I lift my knight. I put it down.
I lift it up again. I frown.
Is this the move? Or is it that?
My heart is beating, rat-a-tat.

My Zeidy's voice, so calm, so slow,
comes back to me: "Before you go,
look at the whole board, not one piece.
Then breathe. Then let the worry cease."

I breathe. I look. And then I see:
his bishop, waiting, watching me!
I would have lost my queen, my pride,
if I had moved without a guide.

So I move my rook instead, and smile.
The game goes on a little while.
And when it's done, I shake his hand.
"Good game," I say. He says, "Well planned."
--- words
rival: the person you are playing against
bishop: a chess piece that moves on a slant
cease: to stop
