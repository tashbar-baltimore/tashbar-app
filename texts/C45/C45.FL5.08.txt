Mr. Halpern's classroom, the week before the science fair. Mr. Halpern holds a list.

MR HALPERN: This year, everyone works with a partner. Binyamin Yusupov, your partner is Tzvi Hirsch Gold.

TZVI HIRSCH: Binyamin? Mr. Halpern, he's the slowest person in Baltimore!

BINYAMIN: I am not slow. I am careful.

TZVI HIRSCH: Careful is slow wearing a nice hat.

MR HALPERN: Then you will both learn something. Your project: build a tower out of paper, as tall as a ruler, that holds one pound of pennies on top.

NARRATOR: The next day, in Tzvi Hirsch's kitchen, behind the bakery.

TZVI HIRSCH: Okay, I've got it! We roll the paper into tubes, glue them, done. Let's go!

BINYAMIN: Wait. Before we glue anything, let's test one tube.

TZVI HIRSCH: Test? Why test? Tubes are strong. Everybody knows that.
===
BINYAMIN: Then the test will be quick.

NARRATOR: They test one tube. It bends in the middle and folds like a sad noodle.

TZVI HIRSCH: Okay. Okay! Maybe we test.

NARRATOR: For three days, they test. Binyamin writes every result in neat columns. Tzvi Hirsch tries a new idea every ten minutes.

BINYAMIN: That one fell. Write it down.

TZVI HIRSCH: Triangles! What if we fold the paper into triangles?

BINYAMIN: That is a very fast idea. Let's test it carefully.

NARRATOR: The triangles hold half a pound. Then a whole pound. Then a pound and a half!

TZVI HIRSCH: Binyamin, we did it! Your slow way works!

BINYAMIN: No. Your fast idea works. My slow way only proved it.

NARRATOR: At the science fair, their tower holds two whole pounds of pennies. Mr. Halpern smiles at the list in his hand.

MR HALPERN: So, gentlemen. What did you learn?

TZVI HIRSCH: That careful is not slow.

BINYAMIN: And that fast is not foolish. At least, not every time.
--- words
partner: a person you work with on a job
project: a big piece of work that takes planning
columns: lists written from top to bottom
proved: showed for sure that it is true
foolish: not wise; silly
