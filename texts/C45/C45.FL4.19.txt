title: Bridges
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: Bridges
source: original
---
A log across a little stream
is just a bridge, a simple beam.
Dovid walks it, arms out wide,
and hops down on the other side.

A stone bridge bends in one round arch.
The heavy stones press in and march.
Each stone leans hard against its friend,
so none can fall from end to end.

But look! Across the Chesapeake,
a bridge so long it makes you speak
in whispers: cables, long and fine,
that swing like giant fishing line.

The road hangs down from cables strong,
held up by towers, high and long.
Avrumi counts the miles roll by:
"Four miles of bridge, up in the sky!"

A beam, an arch, a hanging road:
each bridge was made to hold a load.
And every bridge, from stream to bay,
helps someone get across today.
--- words
beam: a long, straight piece that lies across a gap
arch: a curved shape that holds weight
cables: thick ropes made of twisted wire
