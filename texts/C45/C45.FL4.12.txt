title: The Snow Day Phone Call (a play)
grade: 4
kind: play
topic: Weather and Storms
source: original
---
Six o'clock on a dark winter morning. The phone rings in the Katz kitchen. Avrumi, in his pajamas, runs to get it. His Abba follows, half asleep.

AVRUMI: Hello? Katz family!

RABBI FELDMAN: Good morning, Avrumi. This is Rabbi Feldman. Is your Abba awake?

AVRUMI: He is now! Abba, it's the rebbe!

ABBA: Good morning, Rabbi Feldman. Is everything all right?

RABBI FELDMAN: Everything is fine, baruch Hashem. But the weather report says ten inches of snow by noon. The yeshiva will be closed today.

AVRUMI: Closed? A snow day? A real snow day?

ABBA: Avrumi, please. Let the rebbe finish.

RABBI FELDMAN: There is one more thing. I need your help, Avrumi. Please call the next family on the phone list, the Golds, and give them the message. Then they call the next family, and so on.
===
AVRUMI: Like a chain! Like the paper chains in the sukkah!

RABBI FELDMAN: Exactly like that. Tell them three things. One: no yeshiva today. Two: stay off the roads. Three: we will learn on the phone at ten o'clock.

AVRUMI: Learn on the phone? On a snow day?

RABBI FELDMAN: On a snow day, Avrumi, the snow takes a day off from school. The Torah does not.

ABBA: The rebbe is right. Now go call the Golds. And speak slowly and clearly!

NARRATOR: Avrumi dials. The phone rings once, twice, three times.

TZVI HIRSCH: Hello? Gold's Bakery and Gold family. We are closed.

AVRUMI: Tzvi Hirsch, it's me! Listen carefully. Number one: no yeshiva today!

TZVI HIRSCH: YES!

AVRUMI: Number two: stay off the roads. Number three: we learn on the phone at ten.

TZVI HIRSCH: Oh. Well. Two out of three is still very good news!
--- words
pajamas: soft clothes you sleep in
yeshiva: a school where boys learn Torah
clearly: in a way that is easy to hear and understand
