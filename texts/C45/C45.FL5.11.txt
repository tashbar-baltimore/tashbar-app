title: The Tide
grade: 5
kind: poem
topic: Oceans
source: original
---
Twice a day, the sea comes in,
creeping up the sand,
covering the shells and stones,
swallowing the land.

Twice a day, the sea goes out,
pulling back its blue,
leaving pools of silver light
where little crabs come through.

Who is pulling? Who is pushing,
far above the foam?
Not a giant, not a wind,
not a whale come home.

It's the moon! The quiet moon,
hanging in the sky,
tugging at the ocean's coat
as it passes by.

So when I stand upon the shore
and feel the water flow,
I think of all the hidden pulls
that move the world below:

the moon that pulls the ocean's tide,
the sun that warms the sea,
and Hashem, who made them every one,
and keeps them pulling, free.
--- words
creeping: moving slowly and quietly
foam: white bubbles on top of the water
tugging: pulling again and again
