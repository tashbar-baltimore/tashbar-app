title: The Sukkah in the Wind
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: Glen Avenue Stories
source: original
pic: core-c45-p-sukkah.webp
---
The wind came running down our street.
It tugged the schach. It shook the sheet.
It rattled every paper chain
and whistled, "I will come again!"

Abba said, "Boys, hold the wall!
Don't let our pretty sukkah fall!"
So Shimmy grabbed the left side tight,
and I held on with all my might.

The lanterns swung. The pictures flew.
My hat went sailing, and my shoe!
The wind went "WHOOSH!" The wind went "ROAR!"
It banged against the sukkah door.

And then, as quickly as it came,
it left. The street was not the same:
leaves everywhere, a branch, a pail,
and yet our sukkah did not fail!

We fixed the chains. We found my hat.
We found my shoe beside the mat.
That night we sang a happy song
inside our sukkah, all night long.
--- words
tugged: pulled hard
schach: the branches or mats that cover a sukkah roof
rattled: shook and made small knocking sounds
whistled: made a high sound by blowing
might: strength
