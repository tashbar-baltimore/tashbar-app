title: The Hoses That Did Not Fit (a play)
grade: 5
kind: play
topic: The Great Baltimore Fire
source: original
---
Sunday, February 7, 1904, early afternoon. Downtown Baltimore is burning. Smoke fills the sky. A train has just arrived from Washington, carrying fire engines and firemen to help. A Baltimore fire captain runs to meet them.

NARRATOR: The fire began that morning in the basement of a big store. A strong wind pushed it from building to building. Now whole blocks are burning, and Baltimore has asked other cities for help.

CAPTAIN: Thank goodness you're here! We need every engine we have!

WASHINGTON FIREMAN: We came as fast as the train could go. Where do you want us?

CAPTAIN: Right here, on this corner. There's a hydrant. Hook up your hose and start pumping!

NARRATOR: The Washington firemen pull their hose to the hydrant. They try to screw it on. They turn it. They turn it again.
===
WASHINGTON FIREMAN: Captain, it won't fit!

CAPTAIN: What do you mean, it won't fit?

WASHINGTON FIREMAN: Your hydrants are a different size from ours! Our hose doesn't fit your hydrant!

CAPTAIN: Then tie it on! Wrap it! Do anything!

NARRATOR: The firemen wrap the joints with rags and canvas. Water sprays out everywhere. Only a little reaches the fire.

WASHINGTON FIREMAN: It's no good, Captain. We're losing most of the water!

NARRATOR: Later that day, firemen come from Philadelphia, New York and other cities. Many of their hoses do not fit either. The fire burns for about thirty hours. By the time it stops, at the Jones Falls river, more than fifteen hundred buildings are gone.

CAPTAIN: All these brave men, and all this water, and we couldn't use it.

NARRATOR: After the fire, the people who make fire hydrants and hoses got together. Slowly, cities across the country began to use the same size. Baltimore's hardest day helped make every city a little safer.
--- words
hydrant: a big pipe on the street where firemen get water
pumping: pushing water through a pipe or hose
canvas: a thick, strong cloth
joints: the places where two parts connect
