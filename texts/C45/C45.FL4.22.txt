title: The Pencil and the Eraser
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: Poems, Plays and Fables
source: original
---
The pencil said, "I'm very proud.
I draw, I write, I think out loud.
I make the letters, tall and neat.
Without me, nothing is complete!"

The eraser laughed, so soft and pink,
"Not so fast, my friend. Just think:
when you write 'cat' and mean to write 'hat,'
who comes along and fixes that?"

The pencil frowned. "Well, that is true.
I make mistakes. A few. Or two."
"A few?" the eraser said. "Oh, my!
I've fixed a hundred, by and by!"

They argued till the end of day,
until a boy came by to say,
"You both are good, and here is why:
one writes the words, one helps them try."

So now they share a little home,
inside a pencil case of chrome,
the pencil writing, bold and free,
the eraser close as friends can be.
--- words
proud: feeling good about yourself
complete: whole, with nothing missing
argued: said they did not agree, again and again
chrome: shiny silver metal
