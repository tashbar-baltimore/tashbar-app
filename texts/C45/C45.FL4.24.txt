title: Busy Bee
grade: 4
kind: poem

---
Out of the hive at the break of day,
a little bee is on her way.
Over the fence and over the wall,
past the oak tree, wide and tall.

She finds a flower, white and sweet,
and lands on it on six small feet.
She sips the nectar, slow and deep,
while all the world is still asleep.

Then home she flies, as fast as light,
and dances, left and dances, right!
"Follow me!" her dancing said,
"to the clover, white and red!"

So off they zoom, a busy band,
across the field, across the land.
They work all day, they never stop,
to fill the honeycomb to the top.

And when I dip my apple slice
in honey, golden, sweet and nice,
I think of every buzzing bee
who flew so far, for you and me.
--- words
nectar: the sweet juice inside a flower
clover: a small plant with round flowers that bees love
honeycomb: the wax rooms where bees keep their honey
