Up the ladder, through the square,
into dusty, quiet air,
where the boxes, stacked and old,
keep the stories no one told.

Here a trunk with leather straps,
here a pile of folded maps,
here a hatbox, round and blue,
holding Zeidy's first small shoe.

Every box a locked-up door.
Every lid a "Tell me more!"
Letters tied with faded string,
stamps with lions, ships, a king.

Zeidy says the attic keeps
all the family while it sleeps:
every journey, every name,
every Chanukah's small flame.

So I climb, and so I look,
like a reader with a book,
turning pages made of wood,
finding what is old, and good.
--- words
leather: strong material made from the skin of a cow
faded: lighter in color because it is old
journey: a long trip from one place to another
