title: Counting the Thunder
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: Weather and Storms
source: original
---
The sky turned dark on Friday night,
the candles in the window bright.
Then far away, a silver crack:
the lightning flashed, and darkness back.

"Now count!" said Abba. "One, two, three,
four, five..." and then, so suddenly,
a rumble rolled across the town,
a giant's drum, a growling sound.

"Five seconds," Abba said. "You see?
The storm is one mile off, maybe.
The light comes fast, as fast can be.
The sound walks slow, like you and me."

We counted, flash by flash, all night:
four seconds, three, then two. "Hold tight!"
Then one. Then CRASH! The windows shook!
My little brother dropped his book.

But then the numbers grew and grew:
three, five, then seven, nine, and through
to ten! The storm was moving on.
By Shabbos morning, it was gone.
--- words
rumble: a long, low, rolling sound
growling: making a deep, angry sound, like a dog
suddenly: very quickly, with no warning
