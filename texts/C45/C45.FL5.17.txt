title: Extra! Extra!
grade: 5
kind: speech
topic: The Great Baltimore Fire
source: original
---
Extra! Extra! Read all about it! Great fire in Baltimore! Get your paper here, only two cents!

Step right up, mister! You want to know what happened? I'll tell you, and then you'll want to buy the paper. Here it is.

On Sunday morning, at about ten minutes to eleven, smoke came pouring out of the Hurst building, a big dry-goods store downtown. The firemen ran in. And then, boom! The whole building blew up. Flames jumped out of every window. And the wind, that terrible wind, picked up the sparks and carried them from roof to roof, faster than a horse can run!

For two days, mister, two whole days, the fire ate up the city. Banks, hotels, newspapers, stores, all gone! Fifteen hundred buildings! A hundred and forty acres! You could stand on Federal Hill and see the whole downtown glowing red like the inside of a stove.
===
Firemen came from Washington. From Philadelphia. From New York. Eleven hundred firemen and more, working all night in the freezing cold, with ice on their coats and soot on their faces. Some of them did not sleep for thirty hours!

And how did it stop? The wind changed, the firemen made a stand at the Jones Falls, and finally, finally, the flames could not cross the water.

Was anybody killed? That's the miracle, mister. In the whole terrible fire, almost nobody died. A whole city burned, and the people walked away.

Now the mayor says Baltimore will build again, better than before. Wider streets! Stronger buildings! Hoses that fit! You can read every word of it, right here.

Extra! Extra! Great fire in Baltimore! Two cents, mister? Thank you kindly! Extra! Extra!
--- words
dry-goods: cloth, clothing and other things sold by the yard or piece
soot: black powder left by smoke
miracle: a wonderful thing that seems impossible
mayor: the leader of a city
