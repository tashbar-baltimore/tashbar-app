id: C45.FL4.05
title: Desert Night
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: Deserts
source: original
---
All day the desert burns and glows,
the sand too hot for feet or toes.
The lizard hides beneath a stone.
The hawk is circling, high, alone.

Then slowly, slowly, sinks the sun.
The long and burning day is done.
The sky turns orange, pink and red,
and purple shadows spread instead.

Now cool winds whisper through the sand.
The fox comes out across the land.
The owl wakes up. The beetles creep.
The desert does not go to sleep!

And high above, so clear, so bright,
a million stars come out at night,
more stars than any boy could count,
above the sand and rocky mount.
--- words
glows: shines with a warm light
lizard: a small animal with scales and a long tail
circling: flying around and around
whisper: to speak very softly
beetles: small insects with hard, shiny backs
creep: to move slowly and quietly
