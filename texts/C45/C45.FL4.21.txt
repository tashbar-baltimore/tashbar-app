title: The Chanukah Play Rehearsal (a play)
grade: 4
kind: play
topic: Poems, Plays and Fables
source: original
---
The yeshiva lunchroom, one week before Chanukah. Chairs are pushed against the wall. Rabbi Feldman holds a script. The boys stand on a small stage made of tables.

RABBI FELDMAN: Boys, in one week, every parent in the school will watch our Chanukah play. Today, we read it from the top. Yehuda, you are Yehuda HaMaccabi. Begin.

YEHUDA: Brothers! The Greeks have filled the Beis Hamikdash with idols! Who is ready to stand with me?

TZVI HIRSCH: I am ready! I am ready! I am so ready!

RABBI FELDMAN: Tzvi Hirsch, you only have one "I am ready." Not three.

TZVI HIRSCH: But I am very ready.

AVRUMI: He really is. He has been practicing in the bakery all week. The customers are scared of him.

RABBI FELDMAN: Then save it for the play. Dovid, the oil.

DOVID: We searched every corner. We found only one small jar of pure oil, with the seal of the Kohen Gadol.
===
RABBI FELDMAN: Good. Now slower, Dovid. Let the audience feel how small that jar is.

DOVID: We found... only one... small jar.

RABBI FELDMAN: Yes! Binyamin, you light the menorah.

BINYAMIN: It is enough for one day. Only one day. But we will light it anyway.

NOSSON: Excuse me, Rebbe? What do I say? My page is blank.

RABBI FELDMAN: You, Nosson, have the most important line of all. You say it at the very end, when the oil is still burning on the eighth day.

NOSSON: What is it?

RABBI FELDMAN: You look at the menorah, and you say, softly, "It is still burning."

NOSSON: That's all? Four words?

RABBI FELDMAN: Four words, said right, can be the whole play. Try it.

NOSSON: It is still burning.

NARRATOR: Nobody in the lunchroom moves. Even Tzvi Hirsch is quiet.

TZVI HIRSCH: Okay. That was better than my three "I am readys."
--- words
script: the written words of a play
idols: statues that people worshipped instead of Hashem
practicing: doing something again and again to get better
audience: the people who watch a play
