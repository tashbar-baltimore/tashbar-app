title: If I Could Fly
grade: 4
kind: poem
topic: The First Airplanes
source: original
---
If I could fly like Orville flew,
above the sand, above the blue,
I'd climb up high on wings of white
and see my street from such a height!

I'd see the rooftops, row by row,
the shul, the park, the cars below,
the bakery where Tzvi Hirsch stands,
with challah dough on both his hands.

I'd wave to Zeidy on his porch.
I'd see the sun, a golden torch.
I'd fly so high, I'd fly so far,
I'd almost touch the evening star.

But wait! The wind is pushing hard.
I'm wobbling over Shloimy's yard!
Too fast! Too low! Look out! Watch out!
I hear the neighbors start to shout!

I'd better land, and land with care,
like Orville, gently, through the air.
And when my feet are on the ground,
I'll smile, and tell how I flew around.
--- words
height: how far up something is
torch: a stick with a flame at the top that gives light
wobbling: moving unsteadily from side to side
gently: softly and carefully
