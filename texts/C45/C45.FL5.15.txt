title: Footprints on the Moon
grade: 5
kind: poem
topic: The Moon and Space
source: original
---
On the beach at Ocean City,
I wrote my name in sand.
By evening it was gone again,
washed smooth by sea and land.

The wind came by and blew it,
the water licked it clean,
and in the morning, not a mark
was left where I had been.

But on the moon, a footprint
lies pressed in the gray dust,
just where a boot first stepped on it,
as still as old, cold crust.

No wind will ever blow it.
No rain will ever fall.
No waves will ever wash it out.
It does not change at all.

So when the moon comes up tonight,
I'll look, and think it through:
up there, a footprint waits and waits,
for fifty years, and more, it's true.
--- words
licked: touched lightly, like a tongue
pressed: pushed down hard into something soft
crust: a hard, dry top layer
