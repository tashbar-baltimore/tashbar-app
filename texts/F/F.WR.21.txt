title: The Running Race
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
On Sunday, Rabbi Feld planned a field day in the park. The boys were hopping with joy.

The first game was a sack race. Dovid stepped into a big sack. When Rabbi Feld clapped, Dovid started hopping. He hopped and hopped. Then he tripped and flopped on the grass.

"Are you hurt?" asked Chaim, running over.

"No!" said Dovid, grinning. He jumped up and kept going.

The next game was a running race to the big tree. Avi was the fastest. He was running so fast that his kippah slipped. He stopped to fix it.
===
Naftali passed him. Then Chaim passed him. But Avi did not quit. He kept running and got to the tree last, but smiling.

"Why did you stop?" asked Naftali.

"My kippah slipped," said Avi. "Some things matter more than a race."

Rabbi Feld clapped his hands. "Avi, that is the best thing I saw all day," he said.

Then the boys went wading in the shallow stream by the park. They splashed and kicked until the sun was setting.

When they got home, they were dripping wet and very happy.
--- words
shallow: not deep
grinning: smiling a big smile
matter: to be important
setting: going down in the sky
