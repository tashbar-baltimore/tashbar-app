title: Berries for the Babies
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
Chaim had two baby brothers. They were twins named Shmuel and Yosef. Mommy said the babies could have a snack of soft berries.

"Can the club come with me to pick berries?" asked Chaim.

The boys went to the berry patch behind the shul. There were rows of bushes with little blue berries. Dovid picked a berry and looked it over carefully. It was clean. He made a brachah and tried it. Then he checked another. And another.

"Dovid!" said Avi. "The berries are for the babies!"

"I am just checking that they are sweet," said Dovid with a grin.
===
They filled two bags. Then they carried them home. Naftali carried one bag of blueberries. Chaim carried the other.

When they got home, the babies were crying. They were hungry. Chaim's mother washed and checked the berries and cut them into tiny bits.

The babies tried one bit. Then they stopped crying. Shmuel smiled. Yosef reached for more.

"The berries worked!" said Chaim.

"I knew they were sweet," said Dovid. "I checked."

Everybody smiled, and even the babies giggled.
--- words
twins: two babies born on the same day
patch: a small piece of land where plants grow
carried: held and took from place to place
giggled: laughed in a happy, silly way
