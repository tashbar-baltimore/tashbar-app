title: The Gem in the Garden
grade: 2

---
It was a nice, sunny day. Avi and Dovid were digging in the garden by the fence. Mr. Benhamou was letting them plant celery and carrots.

Dovid's shovel hit something hard. Clink! He knelt down and brushed off the dirt. It was a little green stone with a shine like glass.

"A gem!" said Dovid. "Maybe it is a giant emerald!"

Avi took a photo of it with his father's phone. "Let us show the club," he said.
===
In the clubhouse, Chaim got a big book of rocks from the shul library. He turned page after page.

"Look," said Chaim. "This picture looks just like it. It is sea glass. It was once part of a bottle. The sea rubbed it smooth."

"So it is not a gem?" asked Dovid. His face fell.

Naftali held the glass up to the light. It glowed green, like a little traffic light. "It is not a gem," he said. "But it is our own treasure. I will make a frame for it."

Naftali made a frame from a scrap of wood. They hung it in the clubhouse, right in the center of the wall.

Under it, Chaim wrote: "Found by Dovid. Treasure of the Shabbos Street Club."
--- words
celery: a long green plant you can eat, with a crunch
gem: a shiny, special stone
library: a room full of books to borrow
smooth: with no bumps
treasure: something special that you keep
