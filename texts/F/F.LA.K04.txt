On Friday, the day before Shabbos, Eli went to work with Abba at Peretz Bakery.
===
[pic: 🥣🌾]
First, they made the dough. Eli poured in the flour. Abba added water, oil, sugar, eggs and yeast.
===
[pic: 👐🥣]
Eli helped knead the dough. He pushed it and folded it, again and again. It felt soft and squishy.
===
[pic: 🕰️🥣]
Next, the dough had to rest in a warm spot. They waited and waited. Slowly, the dough grew big and puffy!
===
[pic: 🧔🙏]
Then Abba took off a small piece of dough. He made a bracha. This is called hafrashas challah.
===
[pic: 🥖🥖🥖]
After that, Abba showed Eli how to braid. Over, under, over, under. Eli made his very own little challah.
===
[pic: 🔥⏳]
They put the challos in the big, hot oven. Soon the whole bakery smelled sweet and delicious.
===
[pic: 🍞✨]
At last, the challos came out, golden and shiny. Eli's little challah was the shiniest one of all.
===
[pic: 🕯️🍞🍷]
That night, the Peretz table was ready for Shabbos. Under the challah cover sat Eli's challah.
===
[pic: 🧒😊🍞]
"Who made this beautiful challah?" asked Abba. Eli grinned from ear to ear. "I did!"
--- words
dough: a soft mix of flour and water that becomes bread
knead: to push and fold dough with your hands
rest: to sit still and wait
braid: to cross strips over and under, like a rope
delicious: tasting or smelling very, very good
golden: a warm, shiny yellow-brown color
grinned: smiled a big smile
