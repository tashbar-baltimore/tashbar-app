title: Spice at the Corner Store
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
On Friday, Chaim and Naftali went to the corner store. Mr. Benhamou was at the counter. He had a pencil behind his ear.

"Good morning, boys," said Mr. Benhamou. "What can I get for you?"

"Mommy needs rice and a bag of cinnamon," said Chaim. "She is baking a cake for Shabbos."

"And I need ice," said Naftali. "Our freezer is broken."

Mr. Benhamou went to the back. He came out with a bag of rice and a tiny jar of cinnamon.
===
"Smell this spice," he said. He opened the jar. Chaim took a sniff. "It smells like Shabbos!" he said.

Then Mr. Benhamou got the ice. He set it in a box on the counter.

"That is fifty cents for the ice," he said. "Chaim's mother will pay for the rest."

Naftali had fifty cents in coins. He placed them in Mr. Benhamou's hand. "Exactly fifty cents!" said Naftali.

"Nice!" said Mr. Benhamou. "Have a good Shabbos. And tell your mother to save me a slice of that cake!"

The boys smiled and raced home before the ice could melt.
--- words
counter: the long table in a store where you pay
cinnamon: a brown spice that smells sweet
freezer: a cold box that keeps food frozen
exactly: not one bit more or less
