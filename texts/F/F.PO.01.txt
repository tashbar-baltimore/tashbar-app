id: F.PO.01
title: Rain on the Sukkah
grade: 2
kind: poem
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
Tap, tap, tap on the sukkah roof,
the rain comes dancing down.
Naftali looks up through the green,
and Avi starts to frown.

"Our sukkah will be wet," says Avi,
"the challah and the cake!"
But Chaim lifts the cloth up high,
for everybody's sake.

Drip, drip, drip from the leafy top,
we sing the song we know.
Dovid holds his soup bowl tight
and watches raindrops go.

Then out comes the sun, all gold and warm,
it shines on every chair.
The Shabbos Street Club sings again,
with sunshine everywhere.
--- words
sukkah: the little hut with a leafy roof where we eat on Sukkos
frown: to make an unhappy face
leafy: covered with leaves
raindrops: small drops of rain
