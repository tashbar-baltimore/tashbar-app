title: Unpack and Repack

The Shabbos Street Club was going on a trip to the lake with Rabbi Feld. Dovid packed his bag by himself.

At the bus, Rabbi Feld said, "Let us check our bags. Please unpack them on the bench."

Dovid unzipped his bag. Out came a pillow, a ball, four cookies and a big flashlight. There was no hat, no water and no lunch.

"Dovid," said Avi, "you will be unhappy at lunch time!"

Dovid looked at the bench. "I dislike packing," he said.
===
"Let us repack it together," said Chaim. "We can make a list first. That is called planning ahead."

Naftali read the list out loud. "A hat for the sun. A bottle of water. Lunch. One snack." Dovid put each one back in. He kept the cookies, but he left the pillow.

"Now you are prepared," said Rabbi Feld.

At the lake, it was very hot. Dovid had his hat, his water and his lunch. He was not unhappy at all.

At the end of the day, Dovid said, "Next time I will preplan my bag. And I will retell this story to my brother!"
