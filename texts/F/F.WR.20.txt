On Thursday night, the Shabbos Street Club came to Avi's house. Avi's father was baking challah.

"Who wants to help?" he asked. Four hands went up.

Avi's father placed a big ball of dough on the table. "First we are making six strands," he said. Chaim was rolling. Naftali was shaping. Dovid was poking the dough with one finger. He liked how it felt.

"Now we are braiding," said Avi's father. He moved the strands over and under. The boys watched closely.
===
Avi tried it. He crossed one strand over the next. He smiled. "I braided it!" he said.

They placed the challahs on a tray. Avi's father brushed them with egg. Then he slid the tray into the oven.

The boys waited. They played a game. They raced to clean the table. Soon the whole house smelled warm and sweet.

"They are baked!" said Avi's father. He lifted out the tray. The challahs were tall and gold.

Dovid's eyes were shining. "Can I taste one now?"

Avi's father smiled. "These are for Shabbos," he said. "But I saved a tiny one for each of you."
--- words
dough: soft flour and water, mixed, before baking
strands: long, thin pieces
braiding: crossing three or more strands over and under
tray: a flat pan
