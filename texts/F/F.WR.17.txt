title: Knots, Lambs and a Wrapped Gift

Zeidy had a small farm book with a lamb on the cover. "I want to give this book to Rabbi Feld's little boy," he said. "Who knows how to wrap a gift?"

"I do!" said Chaim. He got paper and a long string.

Chaim wrapped the book. Then he tied a knot. But the knot came loose.

"My thumb keeps getting in the way," he said.

"Let me try," said Avi. He tied the string around twice. He made a big, tight knot. "There!"
===
"Now we need a card," said Zeidy. "Who will write it?"

Naftali got a pen. He wrote, "To Shimon. This book has a lamb, a goat and a lot of farm friends. From Zeidy and the Shabbos Street Club."

Dovid was so excited that he knocked over the cup of pens. Pens went all over the floor.

"Oops," said Dovid. He knelt down on his knees and picked them up, one by one.

"Thank you, Dovid," said Zeidy. "Now let us climb the hill to Rabbi Feld's house."

At the top of the hill, Dovid knocked on the door. Knock, knock, knock!
--- words
wrap: to cover with paper
knot: a tie in a string that holds it tight
loose: not tight
knelt: went down on his knees
climb: to go up, step by step
