title: The Photo Contest

---
Rabbi Feld had big news. "The shul is having a photo contest," he said. "Take a photo of something you love. The best photo wins a trophy."

The Shabbos Street Club ran to the clubhouse. "What should we take a photo of?" asked Avi.

"I love dolphins," said Dovid. "But there are no dolphins on Shabbos Street."

"I love elephants," said Chaim. "But there are no elephants here."

Naftali held up Zeidy's old phone. "I know! Let us take a photo of the thing we all love best."
===
The boys looked at each other. Then they smiled. They all knew what it was.

They went to Zeidy's workshop. Zeidy was at his bench, fixing a watch with a tiny tool.

"Zeidy, please hold still," said Naftali. He held up the phone and took the photo. Click!

The next week, Rabbi Feld put all the photos on the wall of the shul. There were photos of cats, trees and a big red truck.

Then he held up the trophy. "The prize goes to the photo of Zeidy in his workshop," he said.

The boys won the trophy. They gave it to Zeidy. "I already had the best prize of all," said Zeidy. "Four boys who love me."
--- words
contest: a game to see who does best
trophy: a prize cup for the winner
workshop: a room where someone fixes and builds things
