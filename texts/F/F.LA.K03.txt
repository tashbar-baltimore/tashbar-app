title: The Kippah That Flew Away
grade: K
kind: story
topic: Eli's First Week
source: original
---
[pic: 🌬️🍂]
It was a windy morning on Maple Street. The leaves danced and spun in the air.
===
[pic: 🧒🔵]
Eli was walking to the park with his big brother, Shmuli. Eli wore his new blue kippah.
===
[pic: 💨🔵]
Whoosh! A strong gust of wind blew. Eli's kippah flew right off his head!
===
[pic: 😟❓]
"Oh no!" cried Eli. "My kippah!" He looked up, down and all around. He could not find it anywhere.
===
[pic: 🧒😢]
Eli felt sad. His head felt bare without his kippah. He wanted to cry.
===
[pic: 🧑‍🤝‍🧑💡]
"Don't worry," said Shmuli. "Let's search together. Two pairs of eyes are better than one!"
===
[pic: 🌳🔍]
They looked under the bench. They looked behind the slide. They looked inside the sandbox. No kippah!
===
[pic: 📮👨]
Just then, Reb Chaim the mailman came by with his big bag. "Boys, is this yours?" he asked.
===
[pic: 🌲🔵]
He pointed to a tall bush. There, caught on a twig, was a little blue kippah!
===
[pic: 🧒😊🔵]
Reb Chaim reached up and gently lifted it down. "Thank you, Reb Chaim!" said Eli. He put his kippah back on his head.
===
[pic: 📎🔵]
That night, Abba gave Eli a kippah clip. "Now the wind can blow and blow," said Eli, "and my kippah will stay!"
--- words
gust: a quick, strong push of wind
bare: with nothing on it
search: to look very carefully for something
twig: a tiny, thin branch
gently: in a soft, careful way
clip: a little holder that keeps something in place
