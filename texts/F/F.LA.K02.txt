[pic: 🌙⭐]
It was the first night of Sukkos. The sun went down, and the sky turned dark blue.
===
[pic: 🛖🌿]
Behind the Peretz house stood the sukkah. It had wooden walls and a roof of green branches.
===
[pic: ⛓️🌟]
Eli and Shmuli had made paper chains and shiny stars. Now they hung from the branches, swinging in the breeze.
===
[pic: 🕯️🍞]
Inside, the table was ready. There were candles, two round challos and a big pot of soup.
===
[pic: 🧔🍷]
Abba made Kiddush and the bracha leishev basukkah. Eli answered "Amen!" nice and loud.
===
[pic: 🌌👀]
Eli looked up. Between the branches he could see the stars twinkling in the night sky.
===
[pic: 🍂🌬️]
A cool wind blew. The branches rustled, swish, swish. Eli snuggled into his warm sweater.
===
[pic: 🐈🌿]
Then something moved by the wall. It was only the neighbor's cat, peeking in to say good night!
===
[pic: 🎶🌙]
Everyone sang zemiros together. Baby Moishy fell asleep on Abba's shoulder.
===
[pic: 🧒😊🛖]
"This is the best place in the whole world," Eli whispered. And on Sukkos, it really was.
--- words
sukkah: a little hut with a roof of branches, where we eat on Sukkos
branches: the long arms of a tree that hold its leaves
breeze: a soft, gentle wind
twinkling: shining on and off, like a tiny wink
rustled: made a soft, crackly sound
snuggled: got close and cozy to keep warm
peeking: taking a quick, secret look
