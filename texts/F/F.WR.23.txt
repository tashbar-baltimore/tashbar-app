Old Mr. Gold lived next to the clubhouse. He walked slowly with a cane. One windy day, his whole yard was full of leaves.

"Mr. Gold cannot rake all of that," said Naftali. "Let us help."

The boys got rakes. They worked quickly. Chaim was careful not to step on the flowers. Avi made the leaves into a giant pile. Dovid jumped into the pile. Then he raked it up again.

Mr. Gold came out on the porch. He saw his neat, clean yard.
===
"This is a great kindness," he said softly. "How can I thank you?"

"You do not need to," said Chaim. "We just like to help."

Mr. Gold smiled. "Then let me tell you a story." He told them about the shul when he was a boy, long ago. There was no light at night, just candles. The boys sat still and listened. The story seemed endless, but nobody was bored.

When it got dark, the boys went home. Naftali took out his notebook. On a fresh page, he wrote: "Helping is the best thing of all."
--- words
cane: a stick to help you walk
kindness: being good and caring to others
