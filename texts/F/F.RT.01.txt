id: F.RT.01
title: The Clock That Stopped
grade: 2
kind: play
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
NARRATOR: It is Friday. The Shabbos Street Club is in Zeidy's workshop. Gears and springs are everywhere.

ZEIDY: (calmly) Boys, my big clock has stopped. Shabbos comes in three hours.

AVI: (worried) Oh no! How will we know when to light the candles?

NAFTALI: (excited) Let me look inside, Zeidy. I love gears!

ZEIDY: Go ahead, Naftali. Be gentle.

NARRATOR: Naftali opens the little door. Everyone leans in close.

CHAIM: (reading slowly) It says here, every gear must turn the next gear.

NAFTALI: (puzzled) I see a gap. One little gear is missing!
===
DOVID: (in a small voice) Um. Is it round and shiny, with tiny teeth?

AVI: (surprised) Dovid! How do you know that?

DOVID: (shyly) I found it on the floor. I put it in my pocket with my pretzels.

NARRATOR: Dovid pulls out a pretzel, then another pretzel, and then a tiny, shiny gear.

ZEIDY: (laughing) Dovid, you saved the day! Naftali, put it back, please.

NARRATOR: Naftali slides the gear into the gap. Click! Tick, tock, tick, tock.

ALL: (cheering) It works!

ZEIDY: (warmly) Now we have time to get ready for Shabbos. Good Shabbos, boys!

ALL: Good Shabbos, Zeidy!
--- words
workshop: a room where someone fixes and builds things
gears: wheels with teeth that turn each other
gentle: soft and careful
puzzled: not sure what is going on
shyly: in a quiet way, a little afraid to speak
