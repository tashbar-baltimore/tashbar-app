id: F.RT.02
title: The Lost Siddur
grade: 2
kind: play
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
NARRATOR: It is Sunday morning. The Shabbos Street Club is walking home from Shacharis with Rabbi Feld.

CHAIM: (worried) Oh no. My siddur is not in my hand!

AVI: (kindly) Do not worry, Chaim. We will help you find it.

RABBI FELD: (calmly) Let us think. Where did you have it last?

CHAIM: (slowly) I had it in shul. I was reading Adon Olam.

NAFTALI: (excited) Then let us walk back the way we came. We can look on the path.
===
NARRATOR: The boys walk slowly. They look under every bench and behind every tree.

DOVID: (in a small voice) Is it under this leaf? No. Just a bug.

AVI: (laughing) Dovid, a siddur is much bigger than a leaf!

NARRATOR: They come back to the shul. The door is open. Mr. Benhamou is stacking the chairs.

MR. BENHAMOU: (cheerfully) Good morning, boys! Are you looking for something?

CHAIM: (hopeful) Did you see a blue siddur with my name inside?
===
MR. BENHAMOU: (smiling) I did! I put it on the shelf so it would stay safe.

NARRATOR: Mr. Benhamou hands Chaim his siddur. Chaim hugs it.

CHAIM: (happily) Thank you, Mr. Benhamou! Thank you, everyone!

RABBI FELD: (warmly) That is what friends do. They help you look, and they do not give up.

DOVID: (hungry) Now can we go home for breakfast? I can smell the pancakes from here!

ALL: (laughing) Let us go!
--- words
siddur: a prayer book
path: a little road for walking
stacking: putting things one on top of another
hopeful: thinking something good will happen
