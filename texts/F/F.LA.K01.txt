[pic: 🌅🏠]
It was the first day of school. Eli Peretz woke up early. He said Modeh Ani with a big smile.
===
[pic: 🎒🥐]
Abba helped him pack his new knapsack. "Do you have your snack?" Abba asked. "Yes!" said Eli. "Rugelach from the bakery!"
===
[pic: 👶🍼]
Baby Moishy waved his chubby hand. "Bye-bye, Moishy," said Eli. Moishy giggled.
===
[pic: 🧔🧒🌳]
Eli and Abba walked down Maple Street to school. Eli held Abba's hand tight. His tummy felt jumpy.
===
[pic: 🚪👨‍🏫]
At the door stood Rebbe Gutman. He had a kind face and a warm, deep voice. "Shalom aleichem, Eli! Welcome!" he said.
===
[pic: 👦🪑]
In the classroom, one boy sat all alone. He looked worried. His name was Ovadia.
===
[pic: 🧒🤝👦]
Eli remembered his own jumpy tummy. He sat down next to Ovadia. "Do you want to share my rugelach?" he asked.
===
[pic: 👦😊🥐]
Ovadia smiled a huge smile. "Thank you! My Saba makes pita. Tomorrow I will share with you!"
===
[pic: 🎵👏]
Rebbe Gutman taught the boys a song about the alef-beis. Eli and Ovadia clapped and sang together.
===
[pic: 🧔🧒💛]
When Abba came, Eli ran to him. "Abba, I have a new friend!" Eli's tummy was not jumpy anymore. He was happy.
--- words
knapsack: a bag you carry on your back
jumpy: a shaky feeling inside, like butterflies in your tummy
welcome: we are glad you came
alone: with nobody next to you
worried: afraid that something might go wrong
remembered: thought about it again
huge: very, very big
