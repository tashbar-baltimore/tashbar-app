title: Ginger in the Cage
grade: 2
kind: story
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
Naftali had a gerbil named Ginger. Ginger lived in a cage in the clubhouse. She was a gentle gerbil with soft orange fur.

One day, Dovid ran in. "Ginger is not in her cage!" he said. "The gate is open!"

The boys looked under the bench. They looked behind the big box of gears. No Ginger.

"Let me think," said Naftali. He opened his notebook to a fresh page. "Gerbils like small, dark places. And they like seeds."
===
Avi got a huge idea. "Let us put seeds by the edge of the rug," he said. "Then we can wait and watch."

So the boys sat still. Nobody said a thing. It was very hard for Dovid.

Then they saw a small orange nose. It came out from under Zeidy's old giant coat on the hook.

"Ginger!" said Dovid in a whisper. Ginger took a seed and nibbled it.

Naftali picked her up very gently. He put her back in the cage and shut the gate with a click.

"Now I will write a new rule in my notebook," said Naftali. "Always check the gate!"
--- words
gerbil: a small furry pet, a bit like a mouse
gentle: soft and calm
huge: very, very big
edge: the side or end of something
nibbled: ate slowly, in tiny pieces
