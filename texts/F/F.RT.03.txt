id: F.RT.03
title: The Snow Day Fort
grade: 2
kind: play
topic: Shabbos Street Club
source: original
---
NARRATOR: It snowed all night. There is no school today. The Shabbos Street Club meets in the Rosens' yard.

NAFTALI: (excited) I have a plan! Let us build the biggest snow fort on Shabbos Street.

AVI: (doubtful) The biggest? That is a lot of snow.

NAFTALI: (proudly) I drew it in my notebook. Look. Walls, a door and a window.

CHAIM: (reading carefully) It says the walls are three feet high. That is as tall as Dovid!

DOVID: (giggling) Then I can be the door.
===
NARRATOR: The boys roll big snowballs. They pack them tight and stack them in a circle.

AVI: (puffing) This one is too heavy. Can someone help me lift it?

CHAIM: (kindly) I will take one side. Ready? One, two, three, lift!

NARRATOR: Up goes the snowball. It lands on top of the wall with a thump.

ZEIDY: (calling from the porch) Boys! Your noses are red. Come in and warm up!

NAFTALI: (pleading) Just one more snowball, Zeidy, please!
===
NARRATOR: The boys finish the last wall. They step back and look.

AVI: (amazed) We did it! It really is the biggest fort on Shabbos Street!

ZEIDY: (smiling) It is a fine fort. Now, who wants hot cocoa?

DOVID: (shouting) Me! Me! Me!

NARRATOR: The boys race inside. The fort stands in the snow, waiting for tomorrow.

ALL: (cheering) Thank you, Zeidy!
--- words
doubtful: not sure it will work
pack: to press tight
heavy: hard to lift
pleading: asking very, very hard
amazed: very surprised
