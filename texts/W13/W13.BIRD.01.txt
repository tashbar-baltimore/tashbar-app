By Mr. Eli Toledano

Winter is a hard time for birds. The ground is often covered with snow. In winter, bugs are hard to find. Birds that eat seeds must work hard to find food.

A bird feeder can help them. A bird feeder is a small house or tube that holds seeds. When you fill it, birds come to eat. In our family, we fill the feeder every Friday, before Shabbos.
===
Different birds like different food. Cardinals love sunflower seeds. A cardinal has a short, thick beak. It cracks the hard shell like a nutcracker.

Chickadees are small and quick. A chickadee takes one seed and flies to a branch to eat it. Then it comes back for another one.

Blue jays are loud birds with blue feathers. When a hawk is near, they call out loudly to warn the other birds.
===
Birds need a safe place to eat. Hang your feeder near a tree or a bush. Then birds can fly there to hide.

Birds need water too, even in winter. A small dish of fresh water helps them.

Keep your feeder clean and full. If you do, the birds will come back every day.
--- words
feeder: a small house or tube that holds food for birds
beak: the hard, pointed mouth of a bird
shell: the hard outside of a seed
nutcracker: a tool that cracks open hard shells
hawk: a big bird that hunts smaller animals
warn: tell someone that danger is near
branch: an arm of a tree
