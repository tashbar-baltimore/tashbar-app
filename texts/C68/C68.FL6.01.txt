title: Proved and Guessed (a scene)
grade: 6
kind: play
topic: The Beacon and the Locked Archive
source: original
---
NARRATOR: The closet, early morning. Akiva has drawn a line down the whiteboard. On the left it says PROVED. On the right it says GUESSED.

AKIVA: (firm but friendly) Rule of the newsroom. Nothing goes on the left unless we saw it.

ELI: (groaning) You sound like my math teacher.

AKIVA: (handing him the marker) Your math teacher is a wise man.

ELI: (writing fast, excited) Someone stole the files! Someone has a second key! Someone is hiding the yeshiva's history!

AKIVA: (calmly) Stole is a strong word. Maybe someone borrowed them.

ELI: (throwing up his hands) Who borrows a whole drawer?

AKIVA: I don't know yet. That is why it is on the right side.

GAVRIEL: (quietly, looking at his camera) The dust on the path is thin. But it is not gone.

ELI: (impatient) So?

GAVRIEL: (slowly, choosing each word) So someone has been walking there for a while. Weeks, maybe. Again and again.

AKIVA: (pausing, marker in the air) Can we prove it?

GAVRIEL: (honestly) Not yet. It is what the dust tells me. It is not a fact.

NARRATOR: At the end of the table, Benny holds something small in his pocket. Twice he almost speaks. Twice someone else is faster.

BENNY: (to himself, very softly) Tell them. Just tell them.

NARRATOR: But the bell rings, and the moment is gone.
