## For the Head of School (plain language)

Rabbi Khaver: **partly yes.** Where a lane really reworked its lessons, the lessons now teach the skill. They open with a clear worked example, the boy practises with his fingers in 3 or more ways, and there is a "Mistake Detective" built on the error boys really make. The Grade 4 Math, the Aleph-Beis and nekudos lessons, and most of the Kindergarten–Grade 1 Reading lessons are good examples. But **only 62 of the 134 lessons I scored pass every point** of your standard. Of the 110 lessons the lanes said were "reworked, 4 or better", **62 hold up (56%)**. The lessons that were never reworked (Kindergarten, Grade 1 and Grade 3 Math, Grade 1 Kriyah) all fail. Grade 2 Math was reworked by Lane B while I was auditing and needs a fresh look.

What most needs fixing:
1. **Show me questions that were never taught.** In some lessons up to half of the check questions are about something the lesson never shows, such as subtracting mixed numbers or angles on parallel lines.
2. **Grade 2–3 Reading gives the answers away.** The lessons print the check answers word for word before the check, so the boy remembers instead of reading.
3. **"Count the pictures" activities.** In these the answer is simply the number of pictures on the screen.

One live lesson had a **wrong answer**: Grade 1 Kriyah "Read real Torah words" marked 5 right sorts as wrong. I fixed it in a tiny "Live fix" pull request.

Picture of the worst screen: `docs/screens/audit-worst-LK.KRI.L02.06.a.png`. Every word is filed under "Three beats", but only חֲנֻכָּה has three. Picture of the best: `docs/screens/audit-best-LANG.GRAM.L09.01.png`.

## How this audit was done
- **Inventory:** I loaded every `window.ONE_BITE` entry from the 8 `src/OneBiteData*.html` files: **204 hand-written lessons**, 196 with activities (appendix). I matched each one to its lane's claim (ONE-BITE-LANES.md "Taken" lines and PR titles).
- **Sample:** **all 110 lessons claimed "reworked / reviewed 4+"**, plus **24 not reworked**: 12 Lane B Math across K, Gr 1 and Gr 3; all 8 Grade 1 Kriyah; and 4 round-1 lessons with no activities. That is **134 lessons** in all, covering every grade band present (K1, B23, E45, M68).
- **Real content:** for each lesson, a dump of the lesson object, its Teacher Time, and **every distinct check question the engine can draw**. I built each lesson 30 times: 6,441 check items over 204 lessons. Script: `dumpall.js`, a variant of `tools/audit/dump.js`.
- **Scripts:** these re-computed every arithmetic statement, hop, ten-frame, blocks prompt, listen answer, `why` reason, sort bin and findall target. They also ran the hard rules: "bite", the Divine Name (stripped of nekudos, plus אלהים), Hebrew in spoken fields, and a `listen` with Hebrew and no clip.
- **Hebrew:** checked by script, not by eye:
  - **Well-formedness**, per letter: a mark is on a letter, at most one vowel per letter, a shin/sin dot only on ש, no dagesh on א ח ע ר, and no final letter mid-word. **0 problems.**
  - **Source match:** every Hebrew token in every Kodesh lesson was found in the pack or source data. **0 tokens missing.**
- **Scoring:** 10 lane reviewers scored every lesson 1–5 on the 9 points, with a quote for every score under 5 and one copy-ready fix. Each got the standard, the lesson data, the full check dump and my script findings (as leads to verify). I checked their urgent claims myself (below).
- **Independence:** 2 fresh reviewers scored **12 lessons blind**, given only the standard, the lesson data and the dump. Comparison table below.
- **Pictures:** I played 16 lessons as a made-up TEST boy at 390 px (`OB=test … play-shots.js`, 438 screens) and looked at every screen on contact sheets.
