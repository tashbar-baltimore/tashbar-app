Picture order YCB-1: "Your Classes & Bucket" (Tashbar 1.0)

30 pictures for the new afternoon screens: the boy's afternoon is a bright path of floating islands in the sky. The camera flies between them. Teacher Time has its own island where the class work floats. Independent Study is a big clear bucket holding his work papers (his bucket list). He pulls a paper out, finishes it, it gets a check and goes to his done pile; when his core work is done the bucket glows and confetti flies.

Style for every picture: Bright, glowing, happy, premium 3D animated-film look (the quality of a top animated movie): rich detail, warm soft sunlight, gentle glow and sparkle, vivid but not neon colours, positive energy. It must feel cool and exciting to middle-school boys (ages 9 to 14), never babyish and never dark. All pictures in this order must look like ONE world: same light (late-morning sun from the upper right), same colours, same finish.
Rules:
  • No words, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere (signs, boards, papers, books and screens stay blank).
  • No logos, brand names or watermarks.
  • No people in this order.
  • Kosher food only (#26).
  • No crosses or other religions' symbols anywhere, including window bars, railings and patterns.
  • Cut-outs (marked TRANSPARENT) must be PNG with a truly transparent background (no white or checkered box), with a soft natural edge.

Delivery: reply with the pictures attached (PNG for the TRANSPARENT ones, JPG or PNG for the sky), or a Google Drive link, using the file names below (or numbers 1 to 30). Large sizes please; we shrink them for the app.
