**The HoS's words (2026-11-24):** "Beef up all their lessons to contain about a half a year's worth of work … the variety of different ways to learn based on proven research … no downtime and fluff and boringness … we want to keep it exciting." Science and Social Studies are **kept, not dropped**. They are built after the core ladders for a band exist. Their readings also count as information reading for the core (prompt, section 1).

**My one-line check for every S PR:** *Does a boy now learn real science or history by reading about it, doing something with his hands and writing about it, one small skill at a time, at the same quality as the core lessons, with no filler?*

## 4. How each S lesson is built (so 150 lessons stay even)

- The **existing skill id** stays, so mastery, the map, placement and every report keep working. The new One Bite lesson is attached to it. Needed tool: `TLCORE.add` must accept a One Bite lesson for a skill that already exists, without replacing its questions. I wrote the Core Growth tools, so I add that first, with a test.
- **The existing questions become the "Show me" check and the practice bank.** Every question keeps its kid-friendly "How to do it" box.
- **The quality gate** (`tests/lib/obquality.js`) applies in full:
  - 2 + 5 activities;
  - 3 or more kinds;
  - no repeats;
  - a mistake activity early;
  - spoken lines of 12 words or fewer;
  - "tap the reason" for Grades 4–5;
  - teach-it-back for Grades 6–8;
  - never the word "bite".
- **At least 4 ways of learning per unit** (prompt section 5), with no two lessons in a row the same shape:
  - worked example with fading;
  - retrieval;
  - self-explanation ("tap the reason");
  - Mistake Detective;
  - dual coding (diagram plus words);
  - predict–observe–explain;
  - teach-it-back.
- **Science reading is real reading:** at least one activity per lesson uses a text from the topic set (`read` in "do", or `proof` anywhere).
- **Every lesson gets an independent review**, 1–5 on the 9 points, before its PR. **Every PR has pictures** at 1366×768 and 390 px.
