## 3. The units

Every unit has, in this order:
1. **Test-out pre-check** (one cold question per small skill). A skill he shows is marked "shown" and skipped.
2. **Unit opener:** the unit's world in one strong, real-world picture (fewer cartoons for M68), what we learn and why it matters in life, in a short, punchy moment.
3. **Small skill lessons** in the One Bite shape. Each teaches on one passage and checks on a **different, cold** passage.
4. **The real reading:** texts from the unit's topic set or its literature, and chapters of the class book, where every skill of the unit is used.
5. **Fluency:** 1–2 "read it 3 times" passages from what he is reading: a speech, a poem, a dramatic scene (listen → read along → read alone → self-check; no recording, no timers).
6. **Unit check (boss level):** 8–12 tap questions on a new passage, mixing every skill of the unit plus 2–3 from earlier units. Passing opens the next unit; a miss sends him to the exact small skill.

**Ways of learning** (prompt section 5; each unit uses at least 4, and no two lessons in a row have the same shape), same letters as C45: worked example with fading (W), retrieval (R), self-explanation / tap the reason (Y), Mistake Detective (M), words and pictures together: organizers, maps, timelines (P), building knowledge in topic sets (K), reciprocal teaching (RT), text evidence tap (E), QAR question types (Q), chavrusa prompt (C), challenge round after mastery (X), repeated reading with a model (F), Readers' Theatre (T). New for M68: **teach it back** (B, the M68 quality-gate rule: he explains the step to a chavrusa or to Akiva, the editor), **close reading** (CR: reread one paragraph for word choice and structure) and **debate** (D: argue the other side's reading of a text, then decide with evidence).

**Ids:** level = grade + 1, so Grade 6 is `L07`, Grade 7 `L08`, Grade 8 `L09`. New ids continue from the next free number in each strand (`READ.COMP.L07.05`, `READ.LIT.L07.02`, `READ.FLU.L07.01`, and so on). They are **proposed** here and are added only the way `docs/core-growth/HOW-TO-ADD-A-SKILL.md` says, after `node tools/core/add-skill.js next <prefix>`.

## 4. How each C68 lesson is built (so about 109 lessons stay even)

- **One Bite shape:** Today we learn → ready check (the skill beneath, with a bridge to it) → Watch me (one worked example on a real passage, one step per tap) → Mistake Detective (the real error boys make) → Do it with me (2) → Your turn (5) → Show me (3 tap checks) → Level clear.
- **Real passages at grade level.** The teaching passage inside the lesson is 150–300 words (a lesson stays under the size limit). The check passage is a **different, cold** passage of the same kind. The full texts (800–2,000 words) and chapters load from job T's content store, never the start-up bundle.
- **M68 quality gate items:** **teach it back** on at least one activity or Watch step ("Tell your chavrusa why that quote is the strongest"); spoken lines of 12 words or fewer; 3+ activity kinds; a mistake activity in do 1–2 or your 1–3; never the word "bite".
- **Respect and challenge (M68):** calm screens, real-world pictures, fewer cartoons; real choices (pick which text in the topic set to read first); a **challenge round** after mastery with a harder passage; **debate** items where he picks a side of a reading and proves it.
- **No giveaways:** the check answers are never printed in Watch me or a How line; no tile order with two right answers; no "count the pictures".
- **Feedback names the step** ("That quote is about the topic, but it does not prove the claim. Ask: does it show *why*?").
- **Every lesson ends in use:** the next real text or chapter asks the same skill again (retrieval), and the unit check mixes it with older skills. W48's quick writes use C68's texts.

**Activity kinds.** Until job T ships the new kinds (evidence tap inside a passage, story map, cloze, put the story in order), lessons use the existing kit (`sort`, `match`, `tiles`, `findall`, `listen`) with passages shown in `q`. When T's evidence tap and story map land, the units that need them most (6.1, 7.1, 8.1, and the long-work units) switch to them. **No C68 lesson PR opens before T's content store exists for the long texts**; skill lessons with their short passages can go first, as the how-to allows.
