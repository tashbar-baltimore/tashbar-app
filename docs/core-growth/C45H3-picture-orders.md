# C45 helper (Books B2 and B5) · Picture orders

Same rules as `docs/core-growth/C45-picture-orders.md`: the HoS's image account makes the pictures (orders go by email). When they come back, the helper checks each one against its order and the picture rules (`docs/HANDOFF-PICS.md`), converts it to WebP (at most 1,100 px wide and 200 KB), and adds it to the shared art library (`art/pics/`, the original in `art/originals/`, and a row in `art/CATALOG.md` with the licence "made for Tashbar Baltimore with an AI image tool, owned by the school"). Then the helper puts it on the chapter's `pic:` line by PR.

**Until the pictures arrive:** Ridge Trail ch. 1–3 and Lighthouse ch. 1 use photos already in the library (`tall-storm-cloud`, `rain-shafts-dark-cloud`, `lightning`, `chesapeake-bay-from-space`). The other chapters open on the Reader's own cover, which shows the title, never an empty box.

## Style
Job T's band style sheet is binding (`docs/core-growth/STYLE-SHEETS.md`, **E45 · Explorers**). Every prompt is: the E45 prompt start + the prompt below + the standard ending.
- **Prompt start:** `Clean graphic-novel illustration, confident ink lines, cel shading, dynamic angle, real-world detail, for a Jewish boys' school;`
- **Standard ending:** `Boys and men only, modest frum dress (kippah, tzitzis), no women or girls, no text or letters in the image, no logos, no religious symbols of other faiths.`
- **Sizes:** book covers 825×1100 (3:4); chapter pictures 1100×825 (4:3).
- **File names:** `core-c45-<what>.webp`.
