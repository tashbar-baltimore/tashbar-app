# Job W13 · Writing K–3: the minutes ledger

These are expected minutes for a typical boy. "Built" counts only lessons, pieces and checks that are live and pass the standard: the quality gate, an independent review with every point 4–5, and seen as a boy. The plan numbers come from `docs/core-growth/W13-map.md`.

**How the plan adds up:**
- small skills (the minutes in the map);
- pieces: K 12 min, Gr 1 15 min, Gr 2 50 min, Gr 3 60 min each, over 1–3 days;
- quick writes: K 3 min, Gr 1 4 min, Gr 2 5 min, Gr 3 6 min;
- unit checks: K 5 min, Gr 1 6 min, Gr 2–3 10 min.

Writing is one part of each grade's core hours (68 for K–1, 90 for Grades 2–3). Jobs F, C23 and G build the rest.

K–1 is lower on purpose: a young boy's writing time is short, and much of his K–1 writing time is job F's handwriting and spelling, which F counts.

## Left out, and why
- K–1 sentence moves (whole thought, *and*, *because*, capitals and periods) and Grade 2–3 sentence combining and expansion are **job G's** lessons. W13 uses them as bridges and does not repeat them.
- K–2 handwriting and spelling are **job F's**.
