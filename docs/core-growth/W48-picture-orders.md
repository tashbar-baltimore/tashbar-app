# Job W48 · Picture orders (Writing, Grades 4–8)

**For the HoS:** copy any batch below into your image tool (ChatGPT). Put the finished pictures in a Google Drive folder and tell the W48 session where they are. It checks each one against its order and the Family Life rules, makes it WebP, and adds it by pull request.

Until a picture arrives, each lesson screen shows a coded picture that still teaches (an emoji scene, a diagram, tiles). No screen is ever an empty box.

## Rules for every picture
- **Family Life (HoS 2026-11-15):** a modest, warm, normal frum family and school life; kosher food. **Boys and men only in pictures** (`docs/HANDOFF-PICS.md`). Mothers and sisters may be in the words, never in a picture.
- Boys wear a yarmulka and tzitzis. Men wear a yarmulka or hat and a jacket. Clothing is modest and plain.
- **No writing in the picture.** No Hebrew letters, no English words, and nothing that looks like the Divine Name. Signs, books and papers are blank or show squiggles.
- **File:** WebP, at most 1,100 px wide, at most 200 KB. Licence line: "made for Tashbar Baltimore with an AI image tool, owned by the school".
- **Style, Grades 4–5 (band E45, the Glen Avenue Crew):** a warm, bright storybook illustration, soft light, clean shapes, slightly rounded characters (not babyish), a Baltimore row-house street in autumn colors. The same five boys every time (descriptions below). When job T's E45 style sheet is written, it wins.
- **Style, Grades 6–8 (band M68, The Beacon newsroom):** a calmer, more grown-up graphic-novel look, muted blues and warm grays, real-world settings (a school newsroom, a library, a street). Fewer cartoon features.
