## 3. The units

Every unit has, in this order:
1. **Test-out pre-check** (one cold question per small skill). A skill he shows is marked "shown" and skipped.
2. **Unit opener**: the unit's world in one big scene, what we learn and why, in a short punchy moment (the big art goes here).
3. **Small skill lessons** in the One Bite shape. Each one teaches on one passage and checks on a different, cold passage.
4. **The real reading**: texts from the unit's topic set and chapters of the class book, where every skill of the unit is used.
5. **Fluency**: 2–3 "read it 3 times" passages, taken from the texts he is reading (listen → read along → read alone → self-check; no recording, no timers).
6. **Unit check (boss level)**: 8–12 tap questions on a new passage, mixing every skill of the unit plus 2–3 from earlier units. Passing opens the next unit; a miss sends him to the exact small skill.

**Ways of learning** (section 5 of the prompt; each unit uses at least 4, and no two lessons in a row have the same shape): worked example with fading (W), retrieval (R), self-explanation / "tap the reason" (Y), Mistake Detective (M), words and pictures together: organizers, story maps, diagrams (P), building knowledge in topic sets (K), reciprocal teaching (RT), text evidence tap (E), QAR question types (Q), chavrusa prompt (C), challenge round after mastery (X), repeated reading with a model (F), Readers' Theatre (T).

**Ids:** level = grade + 1, so Grade 4 is `L05` and Grade 5 is `L06`. New ids continue from the next free number in each strand. Ids are **proposed** here and are added only the way job T's `docs/core-growth/HOW-TO-ADD-A-SKILL.md` says.

## 4. How each C45 lesson is built (the pattern, so 91 lessons stay even)

- **One Bite shape:** Today we learn → ready check (the skill beneath, with a bridge to it) → Watch me (one worked example on a real passage, one step per tap) → Mistake Detective (the real error boys make) → Do it with me (2) → Your turn (5) → Show me (3 tap checks) → Level clear.
- **Real passages at grade level.** The teaching passage is 120–250 words inside the lesson (a lesson stays under the size limit). The check passage is a **different, cold** passage of the same kind. Long texts (stories, articles, chapters) load from job T's content store, never the start-up bundle.
- **E45 quality gate items:** "tap the reason" (`why`) on at least one activity; spoken lines of 12 words or fewer; 3+ activity kinds; a mistake activity in do 1–2 or your 1–3; never the word "bite".
- **No giveaways:** the check answers are never printed in Watch me or a How line; no "count the pictures"; no tile order with two right answers.
- **Feedback names the step** ("You found a detail, not the main idea. Ask: is the whole paragraph about it?").
- **Every lesson ends in use:** the next real text or chapter asks the same skill again (retrieval), and the unit check mixes it with older skills.
- **Chavrusa line** on Watch me or Level clear ("Tell your chavrusa which sentence proves it, and why").
- **Challenge round** after mastery: one harder passage, optional.

**Activity kinds.** Until job T ships the new kinds (evidence tap, story map, cloze, put the story in order), lessons use the existing kit (`sort`, `match`, `tiles`, `findall`, `listen`) with passages shown in `q`.
