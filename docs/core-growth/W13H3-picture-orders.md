# Job W13 helper (Grade 3, Units 4–6) · picture orders

The process is the same as job F's (`docs/core-growth/F-picture-orders.md`) and follows `docs/core-growth/STYLE-SHEETS.md`:
- Orders go by email to the HoS's image account (the address is not written in the repository).
- Every picture that arrives is checked against its order and the picture rules, converted to WebP (at most 1,100 px wide, at most 200 KB), and added to `art/` with a catalog row.
- It is linked with a `TL_PIC_ADD` block in `src/RealPicsTeach.html` in the same pull request.

Until a picture arrives, the TODAY WE LEARN screen keeps its big emoji, never an empty box.
