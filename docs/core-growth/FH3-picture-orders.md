# Job F helper (K–1 listen-along stories) · picture orders

The process is the same as job F's (`docs/core-growth/F-picture-orders.md`, `docs/core-growth/STYLE-SHEETS.md`):
- Orders go by email to the HoS's image account.
- Every picture that arrives is checked against its order and the picture rules.
- It is then converted to WebP and added to `art/` with a catalog row.
- The story's `[pic: …]` line is switched from emoji to the file in the same pull request.

Until a picture arrives, the page shows a few emoji. Pages with no picture in the order keep their emoji.
