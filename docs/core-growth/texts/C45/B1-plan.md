# Book B1 · *The Missing Esrog* (Grade 4, 10 chapters) · plan

Job C45. Units 4.1–4.3. Cast: the Glen Avenue Crew (`docs/core-growth/C45-map.md` §5). Draft texts; they move to job T's content store when it exists.

**The story in one line:** Two days before Sukkos, the beautiful esrog Rabbi Feldman brought to class disappears from its silver box, and the Glen Avenue Crew must find it before Yom Tov without blaming someone who did nothing wrong.

**Theme (for Unit 4.3):** Judge people favorably (dan l'kaf zechus): look at all the clues before you decide what someone did.

**Who did it (the answer):** Nobody stole it. During recess Rabbi Feldman noticed a tiny dark spot on the esrog. He rushed it upstairs to the Rav (Rav Mizrachi) to ask if it was still kosher, and wrote "Back by 3, esrog with me" on a sticky note. The note fell behind the radiator when the window blew open. The new boy, Nosson Abergel, who stayed inside at recess, looks guilty but was only helping.

**Fair clues the reader can use (planted for inference):**
| Clue | Chapter | What it really means |
|---|---|---|
| Rabbi Feldman's hat is gone from its hook, but his coat is still there | 1 | He left the building for a moment, not for the day: just upstairs |
| A faint lemony smell on the soft cloth in the empty box | 1 | The esrog was taken out gently and carefully, not grabbed |
| The window is open and papers are scattered | 2 | Wind; something could have blown away |
| Nosson has flax (esrog wrapping fluff) on his sleeve | 2 | Red herring: he carried the flax-filled shipping box to the garbage for the rebbe before davening (explained in ch. 3) |
| Footprints of wet leaves going UP the stairs, not out the door | 3 | Someone went upstairs (to the Rav's office) |
| A corner of yellow paper behind the radiator | 3 (cliffhanger, unseen) / 8 (found) | The note |

**Chapters (≈ 1,000–1,300 words each; each ends on a hook):**
1. The Silver Box: the esrog is shown in class; after recess the box is empty, the rebbe's hat is gone but his coat is not. *Hook: the new boy appears in the doorway with his hands behind his back.*
2. Seven Suspects: Nosson was holding the magnifying glass; the boys list seven "suspects" (including the wind); flax on Nosson's sleeve; Mr. Gomez says "your rebbe asked me". *Hook: Nosson: "I know what you are writing about me. And you are wrong."*
3. Leaves on the Stairs: Nosson explains the flax and joins the crew; papers stuck behind the hot radiator; wet leaves lead UP to Rav Mizrachi's office. *Hook: through the door, Rabbi Feldman: "Then the esrog is ruined, and the boys must never find out what I did." Someone is coming up the stairs.*
4–10: (plan in the next PR) the boys question, misjudge, apologize, find the note, and learn the Rav's answer; Sukkos with the esrog.

**Words to know (for job G):** examine, peculiar, suspicious, flax, hadar, clue, conclusion, evidence.

**Planned answer to ch. 3's cliffhanger (ch. 7–9):** what Rabbi Feldman "did" was touch the esrog with his own thumb after telling the boys "look with your eyes, not your fingers". He fears the spot is his thumbprint and is embarrassed. The Rav looks closely and explains what makes a spot a problem and what does not; the rebbe tells the boys the truth himself (honesty, and a rebbe who owns his mistake). The halachic details of esrog spots must be **checked by a rebbe** before chapters 7–9 go live (REBBE: review).

**Fluency passages from these chapters:** ch. 1 "Inside lay an esrog … like a soldier." (≈ 90 words) · ch. 2 the seven-suspect list · ch. 3 "On the stairs going up, there were leaves … gone up." (≈ 120 words).
