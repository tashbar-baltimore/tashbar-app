# C45 helper · Fluency passages, Grades 4 and 5 ("Read It 3 Times")

Helper piece from the Helpers list (`docs/CORE-GROWTH-LANES.md`), owner job C45 (`docs/core-growth/C45-map.md`). Files: passages in `texts/C45/C45.FL4.*` and `texts/C45/C45.FL5.*`; lessons in `src/CoreDataC45H1.html` (Grade 4) and `src/CoreDataC45H2.html` (Grade 5), rows in `src/CoreDataC45.js`.

**The HoS's request, in his words:** "beef up all their lessons to contain about a half a year's worth of work … the variety of different ways to learn based on proven research … no downtime and fluff." The plan asks for **25 fluency passages per grade** ("read it 3 times", C45 ledger), taken from the unit texts.

## How a boy meets a fluency passage
Each passage is a short **"Read It 3 Times"** lesson on his Reading path, right after the unit's skill lessons (3 per unit, as the C45 map lists "fluency 1–3" etc.):
1. **Watch me:** what the 3 reads are, and the one fluency skill for this passage (end marks, phrasing, character voices, stress, pace, poetry rhythm, self-correcting).
2. **Do it with me:** the `read` kind opens the passage in the Reader. **Read 1** = tap 🔊 and follow the lit words (the model). **Read 2** = read along with the voice. **Read 3** = read it alone, out loud. No recording and no timers (HoS 2026-11-24). Then one hands-on step on the fluency skill.
3. **Your turn:** 5 activities on the same passage, each a different kind (evidence tap, Mistake Detective sort, find the proof in the Reader, fill the gap, order, listen, story map, word parts, dictation …), 2+ with "tap the reason".
4. **Show me:** 3 tap checks of the skill on a **different, cold** short passage.

Research: repeated reading with a model (Samuels 1979; Rasinski; Therrien 2004 meta-analysis), phrase-cued text, Readers' Theatre (Young & Rasinski 2009), echo/choral reading, self-monitoring ("does it make sense?").

**Why passages are letters, speeches, poems and plays as well as stories:** RF.4.4 and RF.5.4 ask for prose *and* poetry read with expression; speeches and plays are written to be read aloud, and the content store lets these kinds be short (a fluency passage is 150–400 words, read 3 times). Prose stories are 300+ words (the store's Grade 4 minimum).

## Minutes (typical boy)
A passage of about 330 words read 3 times is about 8 minutes at Grade 4–5 speed, plus about 6 minutes of activities and checks: **about 15 minutes per passage**. The ledger tool counts the lesson (6 min) plus the text (reading time once + 8 min). C45's plan had 10 min per passage; these lessons add the hands-on fluency skill work, so they run longer.
