## Across the batch
- **The K book games (`READ.LV.L01.14`–`.18`) are one template five times:** story → "Careful!" listen → 👍/👎 sentence sort → findall or tiles, with the same `say` and `how` ("Read every word. Think back to the end.") each time. They are correct and warm, but a boy will feel the sameness. Vary one piece per book (for example an `order` of 3 pictures from the book).
- **The Grade 1 phonics games (`LV.L02.20`–`.28`, `PHON.L02.41`–`.43`) nearly all open sort → match**, then findall/tiles, then evidence/dictation. Change the opener in some of them (start with the listen or the mistake piece).
- **How lines over 12 words.** About 45 Grade 1 How lines are 13–18 words (for example `READ.LV.L02.20` act 1, 18 words; `READ.FLU.L02.24` act 2, 17 words). The game speaks them after a miss (`miss()` says the message plus the How line). The engine only checks `say`, but the 12-word rule is meant for every spoken line. Trim them to 12 words or fewer.
- **2-choice pieces** (👍/👎 or two options) appear in most writing games. One per game as the mistake piece is fine. Three per game turns it into guessing (see the two FIX games above).
- **Picture names nobody says.** Picture-only sorts and matches depend on the boy naming the picture the way the writer did (🐱 kitty, 🐎 pony, 🧔 Abba vs 👨‍🏫 Rebbe). Pick pictures that have only one name, or say the name in `say`.
- Family life, names (Eli, Shmuli, Moishy, Ovadia, Akiva, Yitzy, Dov, Zev, Gad), food (rugelach, challah, grape-juice Kiddush) and modesty are good throughout. There is no Divine Name and no problem content.
