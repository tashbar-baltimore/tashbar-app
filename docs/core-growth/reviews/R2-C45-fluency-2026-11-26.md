# Re-score of C45 "Read It 3 Times" fluency, batches 2–3 (2026-11-26)

Gap B1b (Review Round 2), from the coordinator's "Re-review needed" list: **#493** (batch 2, `READ.FLU.L05.20`–`.25`, `READ.FLU.L06.20`–`.25`, `src/CoreDataC45H3.html`, `src/CoreDataC45H4.html`) and **#499** (batch 3, `READ.FLU.L05.26`–`.29`, `READ.FLU.L06.26`–`.30`, `src/CoreDataC45H5.html`, `src/CoreDataC45H6.html`). Both went live with scores of 2–3 that the author raised to 4 himself.

**How:** five rounds. Each round was scored by reviewers who wrote none of these lessons and had not scored them before (10 reviewers in all). Each one read the One Bite data, the full texts, the 3 Show me checks of each lesson against their cold passage, and the bridge lessons. Every answer key was checked by hand, and every `proof` string was split with the repo's sentence splitter. After every change, the changed lessons were scored again by a new reviewer. The builder never scored his own fix.
